One of the original [[Twelve Paladins]] who assaulted the [[Cosmic Spheres|Heavens]] together with [[Iomedæ]] during the [[Rupture of the Æther]]. 

From the brief hints given in the [[Three Lays|Lay of Steel]] of the [[Iomedæan Canon]], St. Alia may have been a darker figure, who specialized in subterfuge and may even have been an assassin of some kind before the raid on the heavens with the other [[Twelve Paladins]].

The infamous [[Holy Order of St. Alia]] is named after this mysterious [[Saint]].
