5555-Shattered-Spears, or Dárgis'dárvin'dársæn'dárzulgrváx, is a [[Kovold|Koboldic]] wandering saint-fool-hero figure adjacent to the [[Nachýrian Religion]]. Their stories often involve them encountering some incident during their travels, often involving highly placed [[Nachýr|Nachýrian]] authority figures such as [[Lizardfolk|Highborn Kozarn]] or [[Serpentfolk]], and correcting their behaviour or thought by means of their mystical insights. 

It should be noted that "5555" itself is not a literal translation, but is based on a mathematical error. As [[Draconic]] uses a mixed senary/hexatrigesimal (i.e., base-6/base-36) system, a more literal translation would be 1295 (or 5555₆) Shattered Spears.

As is often the case in [[Kovold|Kovoldic]] oral traditions, they are, even within the same story, often addressed by many different names:
- "Dárgis'dárvin'dársæn'dárzulgrváx", often translated as "5555 Shattered Spears".
- "Kynrkémruk'zulgrváx", often translated as "10000 - 1 Shattered Spears". (Though note again that a more accurate translation would use "1296 - 1" or "10000₆ - 1".)
- "Chykánthrváx", often translated as "a (undetermined) number of un-spears".

Many of their stories cryptically refer to the [[God|natures]] of both [[Tiamat]] and [[Vahamut]], and were part of the [[Nachýrian Folklore|secret oral tradition]] throughout the former's rein.

Some scholars have hypothesized that they may be a local variant of [[Skyplotec the Wanderer]].

# Marrow-like-adamant 

Marrow-like-adamant is the name of the spear used by 5555 shattered spears. 

# 5555 Shattered Spears and the Human God 
Recently, one particular myth relating to 5555 Shattered Spears has regained some popularity, namely one in which he encounters a [[God|Human God]], often assumed to be a [[Protokyriatic Deism|Protokyriatic Deity]] from the [[Foreworld]].

The story itself is of note since it is one of the few stories seemingly foretelling the [[Vahamut#Exhumation|exhumation of Vahamut]]. 

An [[Ildarin]] translation of the myth can be found [[5555 Gebroken Speren en de Mensengod|here]], alongside its [[Draconic]] transcription.
