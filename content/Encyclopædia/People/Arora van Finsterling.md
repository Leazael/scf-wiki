Arora Leandra (Prof. Dr.) van Finsterling is the current dean of the department of [[Hermetic Philosophy]] at the [[College of Elbisborg]].

She alleges that during her youth, she took an active part in the [[Warlock's Revolt]]. Afterwards, she studied [[Alchemy]] both the [[Academy of Weißenfels]] and the [[College of Elbisborg]]. 

Soon after, she became one principal architects behind the formalization of [[Magic]] into the [[Natural Philosophy|philosophical]] disciplines now known as [[Hermetic Philosophy]] and [[Transliminal Invocation]].
