Vladimir [[House Denris|Denris]] of [[Fenwald]] was the Chancellor of the Great Council of (the still united) [[Lethania]] before its dissolution during the [[Lethanian War of Separation]]. Later in life, he became the first Grand-Duke of the newly formed [[Polovina]] and joined [[House Denris]] with [[House Bern]] to form the new [[House of Denris-Bern]]; the ruling family of [[Polovina]].

He was known as being a staunch opponent of any and all [[Revelationary Politics|Revelationary]] reforms. Following his victory as CHancellor during the [[Great Joust]] of  [[1741 AF]], his attempts to revert the [[Revelationary Politics|Apocalyptic]] reforms made by the [[Lethania|South-Lethanian]] states became the casus belli for the entire conflict. 

He is the paternal grandfather of the current Grand-Duchess, [[Adelheid Denris-Bern]] [[Adelheid Denris-Bern]].
