A name for the five common ancestors of all inhabitants of [[Tel Varesh]] as well as many other noble families of the [[Vareshian Commonwealth]]. Their rites are practised by the [[Masked Priests]] of the Primal Ancestors as part of the traditional [[Veneration of the Dead]].

One of these five ancestors is Medua the Battlemaiden, a figure often identified with [[Iomedæ]], a fact that has led to some diplomatic friction between [[Tel Varesh]] and the [[Church of Iomedæ]], and [[Arcadia]] by extension.
