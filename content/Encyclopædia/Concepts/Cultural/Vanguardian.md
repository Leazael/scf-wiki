The Vanguard is a generic name applied to several contemporary and historical subcultures who live of the forefront of art, culture, music, and sometimes even [[Natural Philosophy|philosophy]].

Modern Vanguardians are generally associated with academic life-styles (or imitations thereof), experimental music (with often [[Ordinator|Ordinatoric]] or [[Nachýr|Nachýrian]] influences), and idiosyncratic modes of dress.

Vanugardians generally ascribe to vaguely [[Revelationary Politics|Apocalyptic]] or even [[Merovianism|Merovian]] world-views, though they are often accused of being too hung up on small philosophical minutiae. They often make it a point to disagree with one another on at least some small aspect of politics.
