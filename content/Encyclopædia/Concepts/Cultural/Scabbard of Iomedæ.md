The Scabbard of [[Iomedæ]], is a mythical item associated with the [[Church of Iomedæ]]. 

In the [[Three Lays|Lay of Blood]] if the [[Iomedæan Canon]], it is told that at the start of her assault on the heaves, [[Iomedæ]] discarded her Scabbard as part of her vow to slay all [[God|gods]] who opposed her. However, in the aftermath of her conquest, she still finds herself filled with righteous wrath, symbolised by her unsheathed sword. To shield the innocents from her wrath, she sheathes her sword in her own blood and flesh in her final act of mercy, resulting in her apotheosis.

The Sheathed Sword, or the Sword in the Scabbard, is a symbol often associated with both [[Iomedæ]] and her [[Church of Iomedæ|Church]].

In the early days of the founding of [[Arcadia]], the Scabbard was a popular plot device in several tales of morality and chivalry. The [[St. Uther|Pendragonian]]  [[Scabbard Cycle]] is perhaps the most famous piece of fiction revolving around the mystical Scabbard.
