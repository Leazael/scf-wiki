Transcalorical Induction is a [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophical]] phenomenon that enables a wireless transfer of [[Electricity]] from a central station, a device known as a Reichenbach Resonator, to many smaller dependent Reichenbach Spools (or R-Spools). 

A large [[Commutator]]-powered Reichenbach Resonator can transmit [[Electricity]] for up to 3 [[Philosophical Units|bkm]], and power many household appliances. They are generally preferred to large networks of copper power-cables, as these are often prone to [[Gremlin]]-infestations.

# Function 
The Resonator in a sense "lifts" the alternating [[Electricity|Electric]] field from the [[Luminiferous Æther|Luminiferous medium]] mode to a [[Caloric Æther|Caloric mode]]. As such, the field no longer spreads isotropically and will instead favour interaction with pre-prepared low-entropy gaseous coils, better known as R-Spools.

Activated R-Spools shed a bright colourful light and are often made of glass to show their operation. The gas inside the R-Spools is a mixture of metastable [[Miasma]] and other, rare gasses.
