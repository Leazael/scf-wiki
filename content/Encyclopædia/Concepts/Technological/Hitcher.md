Hitchers are [[Meta|Metas]] who clandestinely, and often illegally, 'hook onto' the various [[Facsimile Machine|Facsimile Networks]] by means of [[Resolite|Metasphyctic Resolites]] and [[Ordinator|Ordinators]]. They are known to possess [[Ways and Means]] to summon [[Gremlin|Gremlins]] into any sufficiently complex networks and machines. 

While some Hitchers are politically motivated, many simply abuse their [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophical]] and [[Ordinator|Ordination]] knowledge for fun and profit.

In [[Kinescope|Kine-Reels]] they are sometimes portrayed as enemies of various [[Sophist|'Sopher Heroes]].
