The Caloric Æther is the medium through which heat or *caloric* propagates. These laws, governing the Caloric Æther, have been recently formulated by [[Alchemy|Alchemists]] who have studied the [[Laws of Fumigation]].

It has become a popularly adopted theory among [[Natural Philosophy|Natural Philosophers]] that the Caloric Æther is, in fact, a fibration of larger epistemological endo-space, lying parallel to other similar media like the [[Luminiferous Æther]], and intersecting the [[Cosmic Spheres|Temporal Sphere]].

Despite the suggestive name, the mythological event known as the [[Rupture of the Æther]] has probably little to do with the Caloric or [[Luminiferous Æther]], though both derive their name from a common [[Celestial]] root.
