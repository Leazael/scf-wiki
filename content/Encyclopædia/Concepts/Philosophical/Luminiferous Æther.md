The Luminiferous Æther, sometimes simply called the Æther is the medium through which light propagates. 

It has become a popularly adopted theory among [[Natural Philosophy|Natural Philosophers]] that the Luminiferous Æther is, in fact, a fibration of larger epistemological endo-space, lying parallel to other similar media like the [[Caloric Æther]], and intersecting the [[Cosmic Spheres|Cosmic Spheres]].
