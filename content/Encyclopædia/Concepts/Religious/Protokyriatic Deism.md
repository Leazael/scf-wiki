A [[Heresy|heresy]] often adhered to by believers in [[Perfectionist Iomedæism]]. The central tenet is that, since [[Iomedæ]] is perfect, it must be true that in the time before [[Iomedæ]], the world must have yearned for a facsimile, as nature abhors a vacuum.

This means that in the world before the [[Rupture of the Æther]], the void that anticipated [[Iomedæ]] was filled by *protokyriatic deities*. These deities were imperfect, since they only incorporated parts of Iomedæ-to-be, but still without flaws, as a flawed being would never be able to mantle [[Iomedæ]] in such a way.

Deities hypothesised to be protokyriatic deities include:
- [[Vahamut]] (especially among the [[Dragonriders]])
- [[Æsir|Týr]]
- St. Cuthbert
- Heironeous
- Pelor
- Ilmater
- Torm
- Yondalla
Some adherents may even worship these deities alongside or sometimes instead of [[Iomedæ]].

Some adherents may even believe that the mortal [[Iomedæ]] never existed, or that the mortal [[Iomedæ]] herself fulfilled a role much similar to these protokyriatic deities, a belief also called [[Symbolic Iomedæism]].
