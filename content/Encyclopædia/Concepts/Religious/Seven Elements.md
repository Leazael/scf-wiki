The seven Elements in [[Tel Varesh|Vareshian]] philosophy are an allegorical atomic description of matter and spirit. They are often interpreted in conjunction with the [[Litany of Creation]] and of particular importance for [[Thaumaturgy|Thaumaturgial Practices]].

The Seven Elements:
- Blood
- Bile
- Sinew
- Flesh
- Marrow
- Skin
- Nerve

Some [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophers]] have attempted to connect these elements with the [[Cosmic Spheres]].
