A belief practised by individuals that stand in opposition to the beliefs of the [[Church of Iomedæ]]. It is often used by members of [[Synodal Church of Iomedæ|Synodal Churches]] to describe practices that have evolved within the [[Asynodal Churches of Iomedæ|Asynodal churches]] that do not follow the [[Dimmerschluß Creed]]. 

Examples include 
- [[Perfectionist Iomedæism]]
- [[Symbolic Iomedæism]]
- Veneration of [[Protokyriatic Deism|Protokyriatic deities]]
- [[Alatrism]]
