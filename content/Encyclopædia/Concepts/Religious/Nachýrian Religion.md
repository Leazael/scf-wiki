The religion of Nachýr historically contained two core aspects: the highly structured and hierarchical [[Temple of Tiamat|Temple]] of [[Tiamat]], and the more syncretic and loosely affiliated corpus of [[Nachýrian Folklore]], relating to both [[Tiamat]], [[Vahamut]] and [[5555 Shattered Spears|other figures]].

In recent years, in the wake of the [[Tiamat#Disappearance|Disappearance of Tiamat]], the [[Vahamut#Exhumation|Exhumation of Vahamut]] and the [[Nachýrian Exodus]], the [[Temple of Tiamat]] has all but collapsed, and has largely been displaced with [[Celebrants of Vahamut|Celebrantion of Vahamut]] as [[Nachýr]]'s dominant religious structure.
