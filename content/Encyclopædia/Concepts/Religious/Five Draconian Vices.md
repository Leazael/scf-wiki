The Five Draconian Vices are a moral-religious-philosophical concept in [[Nachýr|Nachýrian]] culture and [[Nachýrian Religion]], and are strongly promoted by the clergy of the [[Temple of Tiamat]].

These vices all pertain to the particular [[Nachýr|Nachýrian]] notions of property and ownership, and can be difficult to translate from their original [[Draconic]] meaning. The standard translations, as well as an indication of what each vice entails, is given below.

| Vice | Real Meaning |
|-|-|
|Ignorance| Ignorance of which property belongs to whom, and that all property returns to [[Tiamat]]
|Neglect| Treating your property in a way that makes it lose its value.
|Disobedience| Not behaving (as a slave/property) in accordance with your ascribed value.
|Stealing| Treating someone else's property as if it were your own.
|Treason| Behaving as the property of anyone but your owners (or their owners, including, finally, [[Tiamat]].)
