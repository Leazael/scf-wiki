The star of [[Iomedæ]] is one of the principal symbols of the [[Church of Iomedæ]]. The symbol itself was adopted by [[Iomedæ]] herself, as well as her [[Twelve Paladins]] during their [[Rupture of the Æther|Raid on the Heavens]].

 The Star is said to symbolise the first star that fell from the firmament during the [[Rupture of the Æther]].

The symbol itself is that of an upright unicursal hexagram:

![[Iomedæ.png]]

Some [[Asynodal Churches of Iomedæ|Asynodal Churches]], in particular those that reject the [[Dimmerschluß Creed]], have taken to displaying the star sideways.
