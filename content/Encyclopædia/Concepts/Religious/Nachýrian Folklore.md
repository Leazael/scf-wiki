[[Nachýr|Nachýrian]] Folklore, also known ash the [[Nachýr|Nachýrian]] Mysteries, refers to the loose collection of oral traditions and "secret truths" maintained by the [[Kovold|Kovolds]] and [[Lizardfolk|Kozarn]] of [[Nachýr]] during the millennia spanning supremacy of the [[Temple of Tiamat]].

These stories and mysteries are generally referred to as the _chysezvrakr_ (literally: "un-religion"), and deal with the secret [[God|natures]] of [[Tiamat]], [[Vahamut]] and their relationship to the world. 

The mysteries are often cloaked in many layers of metaphor, metonyms and contradiction, and can be (deliberately) confusing to the initiated and uninitiated alike.  

In the wake of the [[Tiamat#Disappearance|Disappearance of Tiamat]], the [[Vahamut#Exhumation|Exhumation of Vahamut]], the fall of the [[Temple of Tiamat|Temple]] and the [[Nachýrian Exodus]], the mysteries have become a large part of the newly invigorated [[Nachýrian Religion]].

In particular, its stories, in all their plurality, have become one of the cornerstones of the [[Celebrants of Vahamut|Celebrantion of Vahamut]]. Especially, the once relatively obscure saint-fool-hero figure [[5555 Shattered Spears]] has become one of the most celebrated characters of the new religious corpus.
