A Dialectic a term used in [[Hermetic Philosophy]] used to indicate a formal contradiction (frequently separated into a mercurial and sulphuric component) that has the potential to be repeatedly and [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetically]] unified in order to manifest a certain desired and controllable outcome (usually identified as the Rebis, or Opus.)

In layman's terms, a dialectic is a framework used by "[[Magic|Mages]]" to facilitate the "casting" of a particular "spell". [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetic Philosophers]] contend that all  "[[Ways and Means|spells]]" (even those cast by [[Church of Iomedæ|priests]], [[Wild Magic|witches]] and [[Thaumaturgy|thaumaturges]]) are the result a (possibly unconsciously) unified dialectic.

For more details, see the article on [[Hermetic Philosophy]].
