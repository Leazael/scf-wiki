Ensorcellment describes the use of [[Magic]] to subvert the free will of any sapient person. As such, it is often amongst the most feared forms of [[Magic]] in the [[Crucible]], more so even than [[Necromancy]].

The [[Six Tenets|Fourth Tenet]] of the [[Iomedæan Canon]] explicitly forbids the use of Ensorcelling [[Magic]] on sapient people, and the [[Book of Canon Law]] makes this even clearer by prescribing a death sentence on those that practice it. 

Many crimes and atrocities have been blamed on ensorcellments supposedly laid by [[Wild Magic|witches]]. 

[[Hermetic Philosophy]] outright forbids the use of ensorcellments. Even the [[Thaumaturgy|Thaumaturges]] of [[Tel Varesh]] do not look kindly such spells, and restrict its use to criminals and other unpersons (such as [[Halfling|hobgoblins]]).
