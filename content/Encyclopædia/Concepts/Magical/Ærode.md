The Ærode is a [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetic]] Aerial Tunnel in [[Elbisborg]], which essentially creates a permanent tunnel of strong winds that pulls air from the south-south-east, and with it the fumes from the industrial blast furnaces, and away from the city.

This both vastly improves the air quality within the city, but also provides a convenient boost to passing [[Aviation|Ærostats]].

Unfortunately, the area beneath the smoke tunnel has become a home to many of the [[Nachýr|Nachýrian]] [[Nachýrian Exodus|exodees]] in the slums south of the city.
