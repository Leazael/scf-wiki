Transliminal Invocation is a branch of [[Natural Philosophy]], and [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetic]] [[Magic]] in particular, that revolves around the communion with, and the invocation and conjuration of, [[Extratemporal Entity|Extratemporal Entities]], hailing from the both the higher and lower [[Cosmic Spheres]].

The [[Extratemporal Entity|entities]] invoked are usually [[Elemental|Elementals]], [[Demon|Demons]] and [[Extratemporal Entity|Celestial Spirits]], and less commonly [[Angel|Angels]], [[Gremlin|Gremlins]] and occasionally (and unreliably) even the [[Fair Folk]].

Transliminal rituals often involve the use of [[Ordinator|Ordinators]] and [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetically]] Enriched [[Miasma]], and often involve symbols and diagrams derived from [[Esoteric Iomedæism]].

The [[Nine Parables|Parable of the Sorcerer]] of the [[Iomedæan Canon]] is generally interpreted by the [[Synodal Church of Iomedæ|Synodal Church]] as an admonition against this type of [[Magic]]. As such, its practice is not taught at the [[Seminary of Aldsbury]], bus has been openly embraced by the [[Asynodal Churches of Iomedæ|Asynodal]] [[Academy of Weißenfels]] and [[College of Elbisborg]].

# Communion
In Communion, the Invoker mentally casts their mind across the [[Cosmic Spheres|Limen]] to establish contact with a particular [[Extratemporal Entity|Spirit]] or group of [[Extratemporal Entity|Spirits]]. 

While doing so is relatively simple, provided the right [[Dialectic]] is employed, this does leave the caster's mind vulnerable to the influences of these sprits. As such, Communion is usually only invoked with trusted and reliable spirits, not too far removed from the [[Cosmic Spheres|Temporal Sphere]].

# Invocation
True Invocation is the act of raising a spirit up from the [[Cosmic Spheres|Lower Spheres]], or beckoning a spirit down from the [[Cosmic Spheres|Higher Spheres]], and align the spirit [[Cosmic Spheres|temporally]] with the invoker.

# Conjuration
Conjuration is the act of binding a spirit into alignment with the [[Cosmic Spheres|Temporal Sphere]]. In the case of [[Demon|Demons]], this generally involves providing a body for the [[Demon]]. In the case of higher [[Extratemporal Entity|Spirits]], this is accomplished by guiding them into the the construction of a temporal corpus.
