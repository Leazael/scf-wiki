Merovianism is a large collection of heterodox [[Revelationary Politics|post-apocalyptic]] civics and [[Wirtship]]. These theories were first penned down in the 1740s and 1750s by the [[Natural Philosophy|Philosopher]] [[Merovitz]].

Merovianism critiques the role of capital, framing many societal struggles as stemming from contradictions between the working class and the capitalist class.

Examples of these contradictions include:
- **Capital vs. Labor**: Workers create value but receive less than they produce, while profits are privately owned despite collective production.
- **Use Value vs. Exchange Value**: Goods are produced for profit rather than need, leading to overproduction of profitable items and underproduction of essential ones
- **Productive Forces vs. Relations of Production**: Technological and philosophical advances boost productivity but do not benefit workers, often resulting in unemployment.
- **Competition vs. Monopoly**: Competition leads to monopolies, higher prices, and reduced innovation, with capitalists promoting nationalism while relying on international trade.

Merovianism argues that these contradictions can be resolved by restructuring social systems to abolish existing class and power structures in favour of more equitable ones.

In practice, Merovians focus on politics that favour the poor and labourers in general and seek to tax and restrict the power of the rich. They often form international movements that eschew nationalism and the concept of borders altogether. 

Merovian parties have a reputation for instability and infighting, and especially [[Vanguardian]] Merovians are often accused of hypocrisy, given their relatively extravagant life-styles.  

# Criticism
Merovian politics have, since their inception, faced harsh criticisms from opponents, even from traditionally [[Revelationary Politics|apocalyptic]] thinkers.

Criticism against Merovian policies often takes the form of "taking [[Revelationary Politics]] too far". 

Some small but vocal conservative parties frequently imply that any and all Merovianism is part of some [[Tiamat|Draconian]] conspiracy which must be opposed at any cost.
