Urth, also spelt Urþ, or Urðr, is the seventh largest [[Planet]] in the system of planets orbited by the [[Sun]], and home to the region known as the [[Crucible]].

Urth orbits the [[Planet|Moon]] Narkin and is itself orbited by the [[Planet|Planets]] Verðandi and Skuld.
