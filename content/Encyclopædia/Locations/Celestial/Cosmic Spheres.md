The Cosmic Spheres are a [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophical]] description of the various different layers of existence. 

Most laymen understand the spheres as different layers of existence, each housing particular [[Extratemporal Entity|spirits]], such as [[Angel|Angels]] and [[Demon|Demons]]. Some posit that the various [[Fair Folk]] hail from their own sphere.

Many [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophers]], especially [[Alchemy|Alchemists]] and [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetic Philosophers]] will point out various flaws in this interpretation. 

# The Seven Spheres
Modern [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophers]] and [[Alchemy|Alchemists]] understand the different Spheres as nested modes of existence that can be ranked from lowest to highest, with the higher containing all the lowest within it.

The most commonly adopted model describes seven of the spheres:
1. The Infernal Sphere: the realm of rot and corruption and decay, as well as those things both lowest and most fundamental to the world. Often identified with Hell.
2. The Elementary Sphere: the realm of rudimentary form, the incomplete, or unformed, and the parts of the whole. It governs organs, and limbs, but also [[Four Elements|elements]] in their most rudimentary forms.
3. The Corporeal Sphere: the realm of plants, animals and minerals, and the body as a whole.
4. The Temporal Sphere: the realm of feeling, perception, growth, formation and transformation/
5. The Celestial Sphere: the realm of the consciousness, [[Planet|Planets]] and spirit. 
6. The Intellectual Sphere: the realm of abstract thought, and the mind.
7. The Supernal Sphere: the realm of the soul, creation, and those things highest and most universal in the world. Often identified with the Heavens.

According to many [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophers]] and other scholars of religion, all true [[Sapient|Sapients]] are aligned to the Temporal Sphere, yet simultaneously intersect all of the Seven Spheres with some aspect of their being.

Some have suggested that the structure of the Seven Spheres mimics the [[Seven Elements]] of [[Tel Varesh|Vareshian]] [[Thaumaturgy]].

Connecting all these spheres are the [[Caloric Æther|various]] [[Luminiferous Æther|Æthers]], the [[Elementary Philosophical Forces]] and, to some degree, the [[Four Elements|Protean Elements]].

# The Limen
Separating the Seven Sphere are the Six Limen, or thresholds.
- The Infernal and Corporeal are separated by damnation.
- The Elementary and Corporeal are separated by vitality.
- The Corporeal and Temporal are separated by the body.
- The Temporal and Celestial are separated by the firmament and the [[Star|stars]].
- The Celestial and Intellectual are separated by understanding.
- The Intellectual and Supernal are separated by salvation (or alternatively, enlightenment.)

[[Hermetic Philosophy]] have long studied the Spheres and Limen, and have employed their powers through [[Transliminal Invocation]].

# Aligned Spirits
Each sphere has many different [[Extratemporal Entity|spirits]] that may be aligned to it. In this context, alignement does not imply containment. Spirits aligned with a sphere, can freely interact with all lower spheres (though they may lack the understanding or will to do so). 

Thus, [[Sapient|Sapients]], aligned with the temporal sphere, can interact freely with all things corporeal, elementary and infernal. However, they can only interact with the Celestial and Intellectual through their minds, and only with the supernal through their souls.

A spirit aligned with the Supernal Sphere can freely interact with all of the lower spheres. As such, supernal spirits can generally take on a body whenever it pleases them (though they may lack the understanding of how). 

Likewise [[Demon|Demons]], the lowest of all [[Extratemporal Entity|Spirits]], are not free to interact with the corporeal sphere, and are thus generally without body.
