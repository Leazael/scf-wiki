Stars are bright, shimmering lights, lamps, jewels, or mirrors affixed to the firmament between the [[Cosmic Spheres|Temporal and Celestial Spheres]]. Different cultures and religions have differing interpretations of the nature stars, though most ascribe to the idea that the patterns of the firmament either affect or reflect some part of the natural world. 

# In [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophy]]
It is believed by [[Astrology|Astrologers]] and [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetic Philosophers]] that the stars are apertures between the [[Cosmic Spheres|Celestial Sphere]] and the lower [[Cosmic Spheres|Spheres]]. 

As such, the stars themselves can function as vessels, beacons or intermediaries for various [[Extratemporal Entity|celestial intelligences or spirits]]. Both the stars and corresponding [[Extratemporal Entity|celestial spirits]] are systematically grouped into constellations, according to proximity and nature. 

[[Cosmic Spheres|Celestial Spirits]] corresponding to both individual stars and constellations are sometimes [[Transliminal Invocation|invoked]] by [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetic Philosophers]] specializing in [[Transliminal Invocation]].

# In the [[Church of Iomedæ]]
The [[Star of Iomedæ]] is said to symbolise the first star that fell from the firmament during the [[Rupture of the Æther]].

# In [[Kovold|Kovoldic]] Traditions
[[Kovold|Kovolds]] believe that the stars are the egg-fragments of [[Vahamut]], scattered across the sky when the Great [[Dragon]] first hatched, each fragment reflecting the light of the sun back at [[Urth]].
