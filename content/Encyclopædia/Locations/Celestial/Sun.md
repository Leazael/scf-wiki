The Sun is a large fiery body that orbits the [[Planet|Planets]]. It is the primary source of light and heat for all planets its orbit encompasses. 

Most [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophers]] agree that the Sun is in some way strongly associated source of [[Four Elements|Protean Fire]], and is an important emitter of [[Caloric Æther|Caloric]]. The sun itself plays an important role in the study of [[Alchemy]], wherein it is strongly associated with Gold.

# Orbit
The Sun orbits the planet once ([[Astrology|approximately]]) every 360 days. As the angle between [[Urth|Urth's]] axis of rotation, and the plane of the Sun's orbit changes throughout the year, the lengths of the days and night likewise vary. In turn, the difference in the amount of received [[Caloric Æther|Caloric]] is what causes the temperature changes throughout the seasons.
