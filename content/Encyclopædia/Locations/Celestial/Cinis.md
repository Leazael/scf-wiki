Cinis was the original homeworld of the [[Elf|elves]], before it was burned by the [[Bondsmage|Bondsmagi]] of the [[Shining Academy]] during the [[Elven Genocide]] of [[1564 AF]].

The smouldering remains of the planet can be seen from from [[Urth]], and is notable for its variability in size. At its smallest, it is no larger than the [[Sun]] and generally its apparant diameter lies between 0.6 and 1.5 degrees. However, in the course of a day, it can grow rapidly up to 3.8 degrees: larger even than [[Verðandi]].
