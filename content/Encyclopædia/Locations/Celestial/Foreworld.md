The Foreworld was the world that predated the current system of [[Planet|Moons and Planets]], which was supposedly destroyed during the [[Rupture of the Æther]] some 2500 years ago.

Very little is known of the Foreworld. The most well-known references to it can be found in [[Three Lays|First Lay]] of the [[Iomedæ|Iomedæn]] [[Iomedæan Canon|Canon]], describing the early life of [[Iomedæ]], as a shield-maiden on the Foreworld. 

It is commonly accepted that remnants of the Foreworld were used to create the current [[Planet|Planets]]. (An act which the [[Bondsmage|Bondsmagi]] claim to have been involved in.) Supposedly, large parts of [[Nachýr]] were taken directly from the Foreworld, as were the cities of [[Sosulka]] and [[Ilm's Watch]]. Likewise, the [[Æthermills]] and [[Walls of Elbisborg]] were in all likelihood constructed on the Foreworld. 

Another remnant of the Foreworld can be found in some of the [[Ancient Languages]] still spoken in the [[Crucible]], being [[Elven]], [[Draconic]] and [[Celestial]].
