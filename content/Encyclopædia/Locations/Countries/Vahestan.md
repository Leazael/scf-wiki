Vahestan is an Empire on the southern shores of the [[Wilted Ocean]], known for its warm climate and deserts. 

While large and rich in resources, the country has lagged behind the [[Crucible]] in military developments. As such, [[Arcadia]] has been able to [[Arcadian Colonies|colonise]] the islands north of Vahestan as well as parts of its northern coasts with relative ease.

The primary religion of Vahestan is  form of [[Iomedæ]] worship. Its capital city is [[Ankhalabad]], a port city on the [[Vahestan Sea]].

# Origin of the [[Arcadia|Arcadians]]
Vahestan is often seen as the original home of the [[Arcadia|Arcadians]], though whether they originated there, or whether they only passed through it on their way to the [[Crucible]] is hotly debated. 

Those who argue for the this fact often point out that Vahestan has had a very long tradition of [[Iomedæ]] worship, older than the settled [[Church of Iomedæ|churches]] of the [[Crucible]]. 

Its opposers will point out that while there is evidence that the original [[Arcadia|Arcadians]] did speak the Vahestan language, old [[Anglic]] has very little in common with Vahestan, but is much closer to a [[Dvårgish]] and [[Ildarin]] with some strong [[Lingua Alta]] influences.
