The Thryr, also spelt "Þryr" in [[Anglic]] and older [[Ildarin]] texts, is the longest river in the [[Crucible]]. 

Its source has been traced to the roots of the [[Göllnyr Mountains]] on the northern borders of [[Ilmbund]]. From there it flows south and west, past [[Alpenkreis]], [[Holmgard]] and [[Eldmere]], before finally reaching the [[Shimmering Straits]]. 

The river has two significant bifurcations. The first, close to its source, is where the Herle splits off from the Thryr, at the former site of the ruined city of [[Ilm's Watch]]. The second bifurcation is at [[Eldmere]], where the [[Thryr]] bifurcates into the Bruch, which later joins the Himm before draining into the [[Shimmering Straits]], close to the mouth of the Thryr.

The [[Ruijnvaart]] is a large canal that joins the Thryr and the [[Krinse]], allowing for river travel, while bypassing the [[Cacotopic Warp]] around [[Holmgard]].
