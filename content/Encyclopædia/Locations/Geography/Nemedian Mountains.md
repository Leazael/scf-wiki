The Nemedian Mountains are a mountain chain that that runs along the border between [[Izmoroz]] to the north and [[Nachýr]] to the south. 

The mountain chain meets meets with the [[Fomorian Mountains]] in the west, with the country of [[Dalriada]] nestled between the two. 

Long ago, the lands south of the [[Nemedian Mountains]] were part of [[Izmoroz]], but during the [[War of Frozen Chains]] these lands were conquered by [[Tiamat]] and have been a part of the [[Nachýr|Nachýrian Empire]] ever since.

The [[Izmoroz|Izmorozians]] have since largely abandoned all settlements in the foothills of the Nemedian Mountains, and according to [[Izmoroz|Izmorozian]] folklore, many dark things are said to hide in the carvers below the [[Nemedian Mountains]].
