The Ruijnvaart is a 160 [[Philosophical Units|bkm]] long canal that connects the [[Thryr]] and the [[Krinse]] between [[Alpenkreis]] and [[Ruijnwold]].

The canal was specifically constructed to facilitate river-traffic while bypassing the [[Cacotopic Warp]] around [[Holmgard]]. 

Its construction took only five years, starting in [[1754 AF]] and finishing in [[1759 AF]].
