The Blightsea, or Venijnzee in [[Ildarin]], is a large landlocked salt lake bordered by [[Las]] and [[Izmoroz]]. 

The sea itself contains a higher concentration of salt than regular seawater and is significantly more acidic. Furthermore, prolonged contact with the seawater is said to create burned looking scar-tissue. Despite this, several unique types of fish and plant life thrive in its waters. 

The sea is even said to house a reclusive population of [[Merfolk]] who have adapted to these circumstances.
