The Thurmork is the name for the large forest between the [[Göllnyr Mountains]] and the [[Rime Sea]], located roughly between [[Draumdyr]] and [[Førnheller]].

The coniferous trees in the Thurmork are significantly larger than most other tree found commonly in the [[Crucible]], with some trees reported to have grown in excess of 500 [[Philosophical Units|meters]].

It is the largest forest in [[Dvårgheim]] and is reported to be home to several clans of [[Giant|Giants]].
