The Shimmering Straits is the sea in between the [[Step-Stone Isles]], and between the isles and the coasts of [[Lethania]] and [[Arcadia]].

It connects to the [[Our-Valiant-Lady Sea]] in the east, and to the [[Wilted Ocean]] in the south.

The straits house various different species of bioluminescent algae, which on dark nights can be seen as pale colourful shadows just beneath the waves.
