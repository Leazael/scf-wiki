The Wilted Ocean is a large ocean to the south of the [[Crucible]]. It connects to the [[Widdersin Ocean]] somewhere to the south-west of [[Nachýr]]. Following the northern coastline eastward, it also connects to the [[Bay of Corbies]], the [[Tagelsea]],the [[Shimmering Straits]] and the [[Our-Valiant-Lady Sea]]. To the south, near [[Vahestan]], it also connects to both the [[Vahestan Sea]] and  the [[Five-Coloured Sea]].

[[Nachýr]], including the [[Lethanian Colonies]], [[Lethania]], [[Arcadia]], [[Ruïn]] and [[Urrun]] all lie along the northern coast of the Wilted Ocean. To the south, both [[Vahestan]] and the [[Arcadian Colonies]] border the Wilted Ocean. 

[[Murænia]] is consists of group of islands set in between the [[Tagelsea]] and the WIlted Ocean.
