The Gulf of Varesh is the large inlet that bisects the [[Vareshian Commonwealth]]. It is further bordered by both [[Nachýr]] and [[Izmoroz]].

The mouth of the gulf is frequently patrolled by naval ships from [[Tel Varesh]] and [[Vered Magal]].

Occasionally [[Merfolk]] are spotted just beyond the mouth of the gulf. though whether these are local [[Merfolk]] or whether the gulf lies along some unknown travel route is unknown.
