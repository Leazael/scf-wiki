The Bay of Corbies, or Corbijnenbaai in [[Ildarin]], is a large bay south of [[Fort Arkys]] in the [[Lethanian Colonies]] of [[Nachýr]].

The bay is partially formed by the estuary of the river Basilisk, and connects the port-city of [[Fort Arkys]] to the [[Wilted Ocean]].

The bay may have been named for the [[Tengu]] that are occasionally encountered in the forests of the shores of southern [[Nachýr]].
