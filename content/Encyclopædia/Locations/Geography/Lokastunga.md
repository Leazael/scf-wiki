The Lokastunga is large inlet of the [[Rime Sea]] that lies in between [[Thrjoskarland]] on west, and [[Nichija-Zembla]] on the east.

[[Dvårgheim|Dvårgian]] sailors fear going there, due to the strong and unusual tides. They also claim that the Lokastunga is inhabited by [[Merfolk|"menfish"]], though the veracity of these stories is unknown.
