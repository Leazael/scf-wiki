The Dimmerwold is a large forest in the south-east of the [[Crucible]], on the western side of the [[Wavering Mountains]]. The forest lies mostly within [[Ilmbund]] and partially in [[Arcadia]], where on the south it borders on the [[Myrk|Myrk-marshes]].

The Dimmerwold is home to several small communities of [[Half-Elf|Half-Elves]], and supposedly even several full-blooded [[Elf|Elves]]

During the [[Lethanian War of Separation]], a large part of the trench-fields reached deep into Dimmerwold. As a consquence, some parts remain dangerous even now due to the residues left by the many terrifying [[Alchemy|alchemical]] and [[Natural Philosophy|philosophical]] weaponry employed there.
