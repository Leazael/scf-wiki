The Walls of Elbisborg are a series of three large concentric walls that dominate the city-center of [[Elbisborg]]. All three walls protrude from the cliffs that border [[Elbisborg]] to the north, and create three concentric semi-circular enclosures around the old city-center and administrative districts.

All three walls have a full 50-[[Philosophical Units|meter]]-wide south-facing opening, and a second less even, bridged, opening in the south-eastern section that allows room for the river to flow. Due to their irregularity, [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophers]] speculate that the river-gates are a later addition to the walls.

# Dimensions
The dimensions of the walls are all highly accurately congruent, to the point where the original [[Philosophical Units|bundesmeter]] was defined as a fifth of the greatest common denominator of the dimensions of the walls.

The outer wall has a radius of 1385 [[Philosophical Units|bm]], a width of 25 [[Philosophical Units|bm]], extends 45 [[Philosophical Units|bm]] above the ground and 5 [[Philosophical Units|bm]] below.

The middle wall has a radius of 885 [[Philosophical Units|bm]], a width of 35 [[Philosophical Units|bm]], extends 55 [[Philosophical Units|bm]] above the ground and 15 [[Philosophical Units|bm]] below.

The inner wall has a radius of 385 [[Philosophical Units|bm]], a width of 45 [[Philosophical Units|bm]], extends 65 [[Philosophical Units|bm]] above the ground and 25 [[Philosophical Units|bm]] below.

# Material 
They are made of phosphorescent silky-white glass-like material, that cannot be scratched, damaged or altered by any known mundane or [[Natural Philosophy|philosophical]] means.

The Walls are in all likelihood made of a material similar to that of the [[Æthermills]] and are similarly speculated to stem from the [[Foreworld]].
