New Holmgard, named for the [[Holmgard|Old Holmgard]] before its [[Cacotopic Warp|Privoting]], is the largest city in the [[Lethanian Colonies]], having outgrown the slightly older [[Fort Arkys]]. 

The land the city stands on was bought from [[Tiamat]] in [[1663 AF|1664 AF]], just after the end of the [[Crusade|Fourth Crusade]]. 

Fore more details relating New Holmgard and the [[Lethanian Colonies]], see the article on the [[Lethanian Colonies]].
