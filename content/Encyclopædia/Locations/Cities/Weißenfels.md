Weißenfels is the capital city of the [[Lethania|South-Lethanian]] province of [[Yrsingen]]. The city is most well-known for its [[University]], the [[Academy of Weißenfels]], and as a source of [[Mythril]]. Weißenfels is, after [[Eldmere]], the largest city in [[Lethania]].

The former [[Great Joust|Great House]] of [[House Winselniß|Winselniß]] still holds much [[Wirtship|wirtish]] power within the city and it surrounding lands.

The city has given its name to the "[[Peace of Weißenfels]]", the treaty that formally ended the [[Lethanian War of Separation]].
