Draumdyr is the capital city of [[Dvårgheim]], and lies between the peaks of the [[Göllnyr Mountains]] relatively close to the [[Ilmbund|Ilmian]] border. The city is home Senate and Royal Court of [[Dvårgheim]], currently headed by King [[Geirvaldr Skyhammer]].

The city was founded some time following the founding of [[Dvårgheim]] itself in [[141 AF]], and while it is one of the oldest cities in the [[Crucible]], it is younger yet than the city of [[Førnheller]].

The city is a bifurcated in an above ground and below ground section, with the former mainly inhabited by [[Human|Humans]], and the latter by [[Dwarf|Dwarves]], though the two mingle in its many industrial districts. The city also houses a small population of [[Giant|Giants]], though these live mainly above ground.

The city is known to export large amounts of ore, mainly iron-ore and [[Kavorinium|Kavorite]], primarily to the city of [[Elbisborg]].
