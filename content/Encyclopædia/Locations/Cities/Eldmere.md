Eldmere is the capital city of [[Lethania|South-Lethania]] since the founding of that, country following the [[Lethanian War of Separation]] and the [[Cacotopic Warp|Pivoting of Holmgard]].

As before the [[Lethanian War of Separation|war]], [[Eldmere]] is also the capital city of the province of [[Hèrlmersc]]. Before the war, the city and its province were ruled by the [[Great Joust|Great House]] of [[House Arkys|Arkys]], though they have since moved to the cities of [[Eijdesburg]] and [[Fort Arkys]].

Besides its function as the capital of [[Lethania]], Eldmere is best known for being the centre of all [[Miasma]] production and distribution in the [[Crucible]], most famously by the the [[LoeterCo]] company. 

In [[1754 AF]] the city was famously attacked by the [[Muræne|Murænes]], during an event known as the [[Massacre of Eldmere]], in which thousands of people lost their lives. 

Eldmere houses the only official embassy of the [[Vareshian Commonwealth]], established shortly after the [[Massacre of Eldmere|massacre]], and has close [[Wirtship|wirtish]] ties with the city of [[Tel Varesh]].

The city also gives its name to the [[Ildarin|Eldmeric (or "Eldmeers")]] dialect of [[Ildarin]] which since the [[Lethanian War of Separation|war]] has spread beyond the [[Hèrlmersc]] to most of west-[[Lethania]].
