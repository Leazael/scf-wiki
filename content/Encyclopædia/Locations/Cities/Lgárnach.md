Lgárnach is a major city in [[Nachýr]] and, among the large [[Nachýr|Nachýrian]] cities, the one closest to the [[Lethania|Lethanian]] borders. 

It was under siege for a period of three years during the fourth (and final) [[Crusade]] ([[1656 AF]] - [[1663 AF]]). The siege was eventually broken when the [[Lethania|Lethanian]] armed forces showed up and repelled the besieging [[Arcadia|Arcadian]] armies.
