Drysfurt is the capital city of [[Polovina|Grand Duchy of Polovina]], and the home of the [[House of Denris-Bern|United House of Denris-Bern]], the grand-ducal family.

The cities lies on the border of the [[Fenwald]] and [[Væringard]] provinces of [[Polovina]] (though it has historically not the capital of either), and become the capital of [[Polovina]] following the [[Lethanian War of Separation]] and the [[Cacotopic Warp|Pivoting of Holmgard]].

In recent years, Drysfurt has become known as the "gateway to the occident", since the opening of the sub-[[Fomorian Mountains|Fomorian]] railway in [[1763 AF]]. Ever since, the nation has forged close [[Wirtship|wirtish]] relations with [[Sosulka]].

The city is named for the river [[Drys]] that runs through it.
