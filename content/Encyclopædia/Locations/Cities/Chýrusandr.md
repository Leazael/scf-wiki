Chýrusandr is the largest city of [[Nachýr]], as well as its capital city. The city is home to the former Palace of [[Tiamat]], now better known as the Mausoleum of [[Vahamut]]. 

Before the closing of the embassies in [[1726 AF]], the city was home to one of the few Embassies of the [[Shining Academy]] within the [[Crucible]].

The city is home to a large population of [[Kovold|Kovolds]] and [[Lizardfolk|Kozarn]] with populations estimated into the hundreds of thousands. It also housed the largest population of [[Serpentfolk]] in the [[Nachýr|Empire]], before their extinction by the [[Withering Blossoms]]. 

1200 years ago, Chýrusandr was an independent city state known as Megelgard. It was however conquered during the [[War of Frozen Chains]], and made the capital of [[Nachýr]] in [[581 AF]].
