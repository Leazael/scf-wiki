Sälen is the capital city of [[Ilmbund]] and has been its largest city ever since the [[Ilm's Watch|Sack of Ilm's Watch]] in [[1018 AF]]. It became the capital in the aftermath of the [[Ilmian Revolution]] of [[1423 AF]], which deposed the royal family of [[Ilmbund|Ilm]] and instituted the [[Triumvirate of Ilmbund]] as the heads of state. 

The city itself is named for the many guild-halls, and those of the [[Great Guilds of Ilmbund|Great Guilds]] in particular. 

[[Sälen]] again became the centre of revolution in the summer of [[1730 AF]]. During an even known as the [[Twelve-Day-War]], [[Revelationary Politics|apocalyptic]] activists stormed the halls of the [[Great Guilds of Ilmbund|Great Guilds]], kicking off the [[Revelationary Revolution]].

Due to its position within [[Ilmbund|Ilmian]] [[Wirtship]], the city is governed by a separate (highly capitalist/plutocratic/kleptocratic) code of law and different tax-code  than the rest of the [[Ilmbund|country]].

Before the [[Great Guilds of Ilmbund|Great Guilds]] came to dominate Sälen's [[wirtship]], it was chiefly known for its claymines and brickyards.
