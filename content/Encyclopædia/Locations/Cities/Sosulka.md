Sosulka is the modern-day capital of [[Izmoroz]] and the seat of Czar [[Ivan I Czarovitch]]. 

Sosulka is one of the oldest cities of the [[Crucible]], predating [[Izmoroz]] itself by many centuries, and hailing quite possibly from the [[Foreworld]]. As such, the city is the origin of much of the culture of [[Izmoroz]] and [[Las]] and has even given its name to the most common tongue of that region: [[Sosulkan]].

Despite the [[Sosulkan Churches of Iomedæ]] having a spiritual near-monopoly in the city, Sosulka still houses one of the few temples of the [[Old Sosulkan Gods]].

The city housed one of the embassies of the [[Shining Academy]] until it closed its doors in [[1726 AF]].

In recent years, the city has forged close economic ties with the [[Polovina|Polovian]] city of [[Drysfurt]], since the opening of the sub-[[Fomorian Mountains|Fomorian]] railway in [[1763 AF]].
