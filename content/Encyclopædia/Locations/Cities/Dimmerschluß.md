Dimmerschluß is a large city that lies along the [[Myrk]] in the [[Ilmbund|Ilmian]] pat of the [[Dimmerwold]], close to the [[Arcadia|Arcadian]] border.

Between the [[Ilm's Watch|Sack of Ilm's Watch]] in [[1018 AF]] and the [[Ilmian Revolution]] of [[1423 AF]], Dimmerschluß was the de facto capital of the [[Ilmbund|Ilmian Kindom]] and home to its royal family.

In more recent history, the city is famous for acting as the staging point for the [[Synodal Church of Iomedæ|Church]] [[Church of Iomedæ|clergy]] that came to the aid of the [[Asynodal Churches of Iomedæ|Asynodal Churches]]during the [[Thanatogenic Ghost Storm]].

Due to its proximity to several [[Half-Elf|Half-Elven]] communities in the [[Dimmerwold]], the city has probably the largest population of [[Half-Elf|Half-Elves]] of any city in the [[Crucible]].
