Dimmerschluß is a large city that lies along the [[Myrk]] in the [[Ilmbund|Ilmian]] pat of the [[Dimmerwold]], close to the [[Arcadia|Arcadian]] border.

Between the [[Ilm's Watch|Sack of Ilm's Watch]] in [[1018 AF]] and the [[Ilmian Revolution]] of [[1423 AF]], Dimmerschluß was the de facto capital of the [[Ilmbund|Ilmian Kindom]] and home to its royal family.

In more recent history, the city is famous for acting as the staging point for the [[Synodal Church of Iomedæ|Church]] [[Church of Iomedæ|clergy]] that came to the aid of the [[Asynodal Churches of Iomedæ|Asynodal Churches]] during the [[Thanatogenic Ghost Storm]] of [[1761 AF]] and [[1762 AF]] .

Following the intervention in [[1761 AF]], the city was the host of the Great Synod that for the first time brought the [[Synodal Church of Iomedæ|synodal]] and many [[Asynodal Churches of Iomedæ|asynodal]] [[Church of Iomedæ|churches]] together to discuss matters of faith. The result of the Great Synod was the [[Dimmerschluß Creed]], that codifies many of the core beliefs shared between (almost) all churches, [[Synodal Church of Iomedæ|synodal]] and [[Asynodal Churches of Iomedæ|asynodal]] alike.

Due to its proximity to several [[Half-Elf|Half-Elven]] communities in the [[Dimmerwold]], the city has probably the largest population of [[Half-Elf|Half-Elves]] of any city in the [[Crucible]].
