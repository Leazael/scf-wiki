Lagushka is the easternmost city of [[Las]], lying on the coast of the [[Rime Sea]]. It is after [[Zolushka]] the largest port-city of [[Las]].

In the 1740s, Lagushka joined the city of [[Pyelchek]] in supporting the [[Revelationary Politics|counter-revelationary]] [[Allied Forces]] during the [[Lethanian War of Separation]]. As such, it still holds a special relationship with the [[Polovina|Grand-Duchy]].

Like all of its [[Las|Lassian]] sister-cities, it is ruled by one of the [[Princesses of Las]].
