Before its destruction as a consequence of the [[Cacotopic Warp]], Holmgard was the capital of the (still united) [[Lethanian Confederacy|Lethania]]  and the largest city of the [[Crucible]]. 

The city was situated at the confluence of the rivers [[Drys]] and [[Thryr]]two of the most important natural highways of the [[Crucible]].

In ancient times, the city was named  _Lethan_, and as such, give its name to the [[Lethanian Confederacy|Confederacy]]. Over time, the city was more often referred to by the metonym _Holmgard_, describing the large Walls surrounding the islands in the river.

Holmgard officially stayed neutral during the [[Lethanian War of Separation]], siding neither with [[Lethania|South-Lethania]] or [[Polovina]]. 

The war was ended in [[1745 AF]] by the appearance of the [[Cacotopic Warp]], known as the [[Cacotopic Warp|Pivoting of Holmgard]], which has rendered the city either lost or destroyed.

The [[Cacotopic Warp|Pivoting]] was preceded by two earlier, less permanent events, in [[1726 AF]] and [[1743 AF]].
