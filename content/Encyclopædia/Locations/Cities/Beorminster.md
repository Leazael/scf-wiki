Beorminster is a city in [[Arcadia]] and is mainly known for housing the parliament of [[Arcadia]]. While the seat of many governmental institutes, it is not considered the capital of [[Arcadia]], a title that is reserved for the older city of [[Covenant]].

The parliament itself was instituted by Priest-Queen [[Mary VIII]] in [[1737 AF]] following a series of reforms known as the [[White Revolution]]. 

Before the establishment of the parliament in [[1738 AF]], Beorminster was a relatively small city that was largely overshadowed by the neighbouring sister-city of Fairbridge. 

In the years after, Beorminster grew quickly in size until it effectively merged with its sister-city. As such, Fairbridge is now considered a borough within the larger city of Beorminster. Nevertheless, older maps may still display Fairbridge instead of Beorminster.
