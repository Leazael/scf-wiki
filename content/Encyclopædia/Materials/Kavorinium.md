Kavorinium, sometimes simply called Kavor, is a light, silvery-white [[Four Elements|Deuterean Metal]], with a [[Four Elements#Protohydric Index|Protohydric Index]] of 13.

While sometimes said to be so light that it is unaffected by gravity altogether, this is in fact false. However, it is still a very practical light-weight metal that is used for construction when weight is an important factor, especially in [[Aviation|Æronautics]].

The raw ore, also known as Kavorite, can be found in the [[Göllnyr Mountains]], the north-eastern branch of the [[Fomorian Mountains]] as well as in the mountains near the [[Lethanian Colonies]] in [[Nachýr]].
