Mythril, also spelt Mithril and sometimes called Hard-Silver is a [[Four Elements|Deuterean Metal]], with a [[Four Elements#Protohydric Index|Protohydric Index]] of 22.

Mytrhil is a rare pale-silver material that is lighter, yet harder than steel. It is highly resistant to corrosion, relatively ductile and has a very high melting point (almost [[Philosophical Units|1700°C]]).

Mythral has found many applications in [[Aviation|Æronautics]] as well as in military applications as Mythral mesh or chainmail offers a large degree of protection.

The [[Myriad Mountains]], which separate [[Lethania]], [[Ilmbund]] and [[Arcadia]], is one of the rare sources of raw Mythril. Especially the city of [[Weißenfels]] has become a hotbed of Mythril mining and refining.
