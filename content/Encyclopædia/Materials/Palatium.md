Palatium, sometimes  called Palatinum or Platinum, is a silvery-white metal with a [[Four Elements#Protohydric Index|Protohydric Index]] of 78.

There are no known ore-deposits of the metal, with all known instances of Palatium objects being imported (in finished form) from [[Nachýr]]. As such, almost all palatium in the [[Crucible]] was plunder obtained during the various [[Crusade|Crusades]] and was the possession of the [[Arcadia|Arcadian Crown]]. The name "Palatium" is directly derived from the [[Holy Order of Paladins]], whose swords bear a pommel inlead with the metals.

In [[Draconic]] the name "vhádanzr" is used for the metal, which is most literally translated as [[Vahamut]]-metal or [[Vahamut]]-silver. The scales of [[Vahamut]] are said to either resemble or be made of Palatium.
