The Four Protean Elements are the foundational building blocks that make up all matter. They are frequently used and invoked by [[Natural Philosophy|Natural Philosophers]] and [[Alchemy|Alchemists]] in particular.

All substances are made from some combination of these four elements, and all transmutations involve the creation, annihilation or permutation of these elements to some degree.

While it may seem that [[Alchemy|Alchemists]] have always had a fundamental understanding of these elements, the interpretation and models underlying these elements have changed drastically over the course of centuries.

As such, most modern [[Alchemy|Alchemist]] refer to Protean Fire (🜂), Protean Earth (🜃), Protean Air (🜁) and Protean Water (🜄) to distinguish the primary alchemical elements from the base compounds found in nature, which are themselves amalgams of different Deuterean Elements. 

Modern alchemists are capable of accurately modelling and predicting the outcome of transmutations by means of [[Ordinator|Ordination]].

Sometimes [[Alchemy|Alchemists]] and [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetic Philosophers]] [[Transliminal Invocation|summon]] [[Extratemporal Entity|spirits]] incorporating aspects of the Protean Elements, also known as [[Elemental|Elemental Spirits]].

# Deuterean Elements
The Deuterean Elements are the first order of substances formed from the protean elements. From these elements, all compounds and amalgams are derived.

Examples include metals, like Iron, Gold, [[Kavorinium]] and [[Mythril]]; other solids, like Sulpher and Porphyrium; liquors like [[Quicksilver]] and Bromine, and fumigations like Oxygen and Nitrogen.

## Protohydric Index 
%% Aquagenitor %%
The nature of the Deuterean Elements is largely determined by its Protohydric Index, that is, the amount of Protean Water present within it. In a stable compound, the amount of Protean Water is equal to the amount of Protean Fire (in the sense of transmutation balancing). 

## Chthonic Mass
The combined amount of Protean Water and Protean Earth is what determines the mass of a Deuterean Element. This combined amount is referred to as the Chthonic Mass of the Deuterean Element.

Most Deuterean Elements contain roughly the same amount of Protean Earth as Protean Water. However, variations can occur without significantly altering the Element's [[Alchemy|Alchemical]] properties.

## Hyper- and Hypopyric Elements
Deuterean Elements that have an excess of Protean Fire are called Hyperpyric, and those with a deficiency of Protean Fire are called Hypopyric. Ordinary table-salt (🜔) is, for instance, formed from Hypopyric Natronium and Hyperpyric Chloron.
