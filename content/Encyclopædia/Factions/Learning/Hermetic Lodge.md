A hermetic lodge is a social club, often, though not exclusively, high-society, that is dedicated to the amateur study of [[Hermetic Philosophy]]. 

Most of these lodges are connected to the [[College of Elbisborg]], or more rarely, the [[Academy of Weißenfels]], usually through alumni, donorship and publications.

Membership of a lodge often requires a previous, though not necessarily formal, study of [[Natural Philosophy]], or sometimes even membership of a particular [[Church of Iomedæ|Church]].
