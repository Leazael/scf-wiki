The Exalted School of [[Tel Varesh]] (also referred to as the High School of [[Tel Varesh]]) is the pre-eminent of academy of [[Tel Varesh]] and the [[Vareshian Commonwealth]]. 

It is older than any of the [[University|Universities]] of the [[Crucible]], supposedly being founded only shortly after the [[Rupture of the Æther]]. 

The Exalted School is reserved for only scions of the [[Tel Varesh|Vareshian]] gentry, who are generally instructed in the Eleven Noble Arts. Both men and women attend the School, though classes are strictly separated. 

Due to long-standing diplomatic ties between [[Tel Varesh]] and the [[Bondsmage|Bondsmagi]], the school has a long tradition of instruction in the [[Magic|Magical]] Arts, also called [[Thaumaturgy]] by the [[Tel Varesh|Vareshians]]. 

Despite the closing of the [[Shining Academy|Shining Academy's]]Embassies in [[1726 AF]], the Students of the Exalted School are still expected to learn [[Lingua Alta]]; a fact that has proven useful during the earliest diplomatic missions between the [[Vareshian Commonwealth|Commonwealth]] and the [[Crucible]].
