The Corridor refers to the [[Revelationary Politics|Apocalyptic]] side during the [[Lethanian War of Separation]] that stood in opposition to the [[Allied Forces]] to the north and south.

The Corridor comprised an aliance between:
- [[Ilmbund]]
- The [[Lethania|Lethanian]] provinces of [[Hèrlmersc]] and [[Yrsingen]], as well as the north and western parts of [[Averion]].

The Corridor was named for being "stuck" in between the [[Allied Forces]] of [[Fenwald]] and [[Væringard]] to the north-west, and [[Arcadia]] to the south-east.

After the [[Lethanian War of Separation|war]], [[Hèrlmersc]] and [[Yrsingen]] would join to form the newly minted [[Revelationary Politics|Revelationary]] People's Republic of [[Lethania|South-Lethania]].
