The political parties of [[Ilmbund]] are organizations that coordinate candidates in [[Ilmbund|Ilmian]] elections, both local and nationwide. While the parties are ostensibly independent from the [[Great Guilds of Ilmbund|Great Guilds]], many of them have close ties nonetheless.

Generally, these parties are divided between Conspiring Parties, which form a coalition holding a majority in parliament, and the remaining Dissident parties.

# Conspiring Parties
%% Samenzwerende Partijen %%
## The Three Hares Party
A political party with roots in the [[Guild of Clockworkers]]. They have a strong emphasis on [[Wirtship]] and entrepreneurship. 

They claim that each of their policies has been [[Natural Philosophy|philosophically]] determined by means of [[Ordinator|Ordinated]] models. As such, they are often called labelled as technocrats.

## Post-Apocalyptic Appeal
The Post-Apocalyptic Appeal is an [[Revelationary Politics|Apocalyptic]] party with a moderate [[Merovianism|Merovian]] streak. They have a strong emphasis on governmental and societal reforms that promote equity and equal opportunities.

## Golden Sun 
The Golden Sun is the oldest of the [[Revelationary Politics|Apocalyptic]] parties. They have a strong emphasis on matriotism and [[Wirtship|wirtish]] and military collaboration between [[Ildarin]]-speaking countries, and have been traditionally a "low tax, small government" party.

Their popularity has been on a slow decline for many years, however, their recent conservative stances on [[Kovold|Kovoldic]] [[Nachýrian Exodus|immigration]] have given them a significant boost.

# Dissident Parties 
%% Onenige Partijen %%
## The Watchtower 
The Watchtower is a relatively new part. They are staunchly matriotic, nationalistic and isolationist; and currently run on a platform centred on violently removing [[Kovold|Kovoldic]] [[Nachýrian Exodus|immigrants]] (also referred to as the [[Polovina|Polovian]] model).

They are strongly opposed to anything that they perceive as [[Merovianism|Merovian]] influences, despite frequently often co-opting [[Merovianism|Merovian]] terminology. They frequently imply that any and all [[Merovianism|Merovianism]] is part of some [[Tiamat|Draconian]] conspiracy.

## The Dug Grave 
The Dug Grave is a party that represents an [[Asynodal Churches of Iomedæ|asynodally]] conservative, [[Revelationary Politics|anti-apocalyptic]] viewpoint.

They favour strong military policies, a return to conscription, and wish to rid [[Ilmbund]] of [[Alatrism|Alatric]] influences. They are quite popular among rural voters, due to their consistently pro-agrarian programs.

They are named for the [[Nine Parables|parable of the Gravedigger]].

## Goatbucks
The Goatbucks are the largest and oldest [[Merovianism|Merovian]] party and include some of the members of [[Merovitz]] amongst its founders. They run on a platform of welfare and care for the workers and higher taxes for the rich.

As such, they are consistently pro-labour, and anti-capital and wish to end the state violence and repression frequently imparted by the [[Miliz]].

Their name stems from a reclaimed insult dating to the earliest commentaries on [[Merovitz|Merovitz']] writing. 

## Stake and Hammer
Stake and Hammer are a radical and vocal [[Merovianism|Merovian]] party. They grew directly out of the various labour and union movements in the wake of the [[Nachýrian Exodus]].

They advocate for full citizenship for all [[Kovold|Kovoldic]] immigrants, and the expropriation of the rich. 

## Spark and Tinder 
Spark and Tinder is a small "one-issue" party that tries to secure more rights for [[Automaton|Manufactured Thinkers]]. They often vote along with the Three Hares party, though they do have an occasional [[Merovianism|Merovian]] streak on particular issues, especially those related to labour and industry, which keeps them from allying with the larger [[Revelationary Politics|apocalyptic]] parties.
