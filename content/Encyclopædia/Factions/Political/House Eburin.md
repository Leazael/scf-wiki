_Motto: Margaritae inter Porcos. Blazon: Party per fess embattled Argent and Vert, in chief a boar volante Proper, in base an oak-tree eradicated Argent._

One of the former [[Great Joust|Great Houses]] of [[Lethania]] before the [[Lethanian War of Separation]]. They used to be the ruling family of [[Thurandir]] and made their home in the city of [[Klingeborn]].

While the family stayed neutral during the war, they formally joined [[Polovina]] in the aftermath. Nowadays their domain has been reduced to the port-city of [[Krelich]] on the [[Rime Sea]], and have sworn fealty to the [[House of Denris-Bern]].

They are known for breeding and riding large winged boars.
