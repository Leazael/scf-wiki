The Allied Forces commonly refer to the [[Revelationary Politics|counter-revelationary]] side of the [[Lethanian War of Separation]], which stood in opposition to the [[Revelationary Politics|apocalyptic]] [[Corridor]]. 

The allied forces consisted of:
- [[Arcadia]].
- The [[Lethania|Lethanian]] provinces of [[Fenwald]] and [[Væringard]], and the eastern corner of [[Averion]] (and [[Maidenshrine]] in particular).
- The [[Las|Lassian]] cities [[Pyelchek]] and [[Lagushka]].

After the war, [[Fenwald]] and [[Væringard]] were joined with the conquered parts of [[Thurandir]] into the new nation of [[Polovina]], under the rule of [[Vladimir Denris]] and the newly united [[House of Denris-Bern]].
