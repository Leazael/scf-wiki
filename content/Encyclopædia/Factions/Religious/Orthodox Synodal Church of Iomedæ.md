The Orthodox Church is the name given to specifically the [[Arcadia|Arcadian]] branch of the [[Synodal Church of Iomedæ]] and governs [[Arcadia]] and the largest (non-[[Las|Lassian]]) part of [[Polovina]]. 

Currently, 24 of the 48 [[Bishop|Archbishops]] in the [[Synod]] belong to the Orthodox Church.

The Orthodox Church recognizes the Queen of [[Arcadia]], [[Mary VIII]] as the formal head of the church and the pre-eminent representative of Iomedæ.

The first split occurred during the [[Revelationary Revolution]] when more and more churches distanced themselves from the [[Synod]] and the [[Synodal Church of Iomedæ]] to form different [[Asynodal Churches of Iomedæ|Asynodal Churches]].

The Orthodox Church was formed after the [[Secession of '37]] in the wake of the [[White Revolution]], with the [[Sosulkan Churches of Iomedæ|Sosulkan]] [[Bishop|Archbishops]] formally withdrawing from the [[Synod]]. However, since the [[Reparation of '51]], with the support of Czar [[Ivan I Czarovitch]], the two churches reconciled and once again joined the [[Synod]] to form the larger [[Synodal Church of Iomedæ]].

In [[1762 AF]], the Orthodox Synodal Church of Iomedæ also reconciled with many of the [[Asynodal Churches of Iomedæ]] in the wake of the [[Thanatogenic Ghost Storm]], after the [[Yádor|Yádorian]] invasion of [[1761 AF]]. The final reconciliation took the form of the [[Dimmerschluß Creed]].
