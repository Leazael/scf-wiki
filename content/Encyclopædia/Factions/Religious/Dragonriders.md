The Dragonriders, or _Æxilrdráucchýr_, are a small religious group who have combined concepts from the [[Church of Iomedæ|worship]] of [[Iomedæ]] and the modern [[Celebrants of Vahamut|Celebration]] of [[Vahamut]]. 

In the aftermath of the [[Nachýrian Exodus]], small congregations comprising mainly [[Human]] and [[Kovold]] worshippers have sprung up in many of the affected urban areas within the [[Crucible]]. Many adherents can especially be found in [[Vanguardian]] circles.

The name "Dragonriders" harkens back to some [[Iomedæan Canon|apocryphal]] descriptions that [[Iomedæ]] may have ridden a silver [[Dragon]] during her [[Rupture of the Æther|raid on the heavens]], a tale that has become of pivotal importance to this new splinter religion. Dragonriders generally agree that both [[Vahamut]] and [[Iomedæ]] are recepticles for the same supernal godhead described in [[Symbolic Iomedæism]]
