The Celebration of [[Vahamut]] is a recent [[Nachýr|Nachýrian]] religion that sprang up in the wake of the [[Tiamat#Disappearance|Disappearance of Tiamat]] and the [[Vahamut#Exhumation|Exhumation of Vahamut]], and that directly contributed to the onset of the [[Nachýrian Exodus]].  

# Beliefs
The [[Nachýrian Exodus|exodus]], which brought together many different heterogeneous [[Nachýrian Religion|Nachýrian folk myths]], has led to the creation of many new and heavily syncretic beliefs among the Celebrants. 

As such, there is no central structure, doctrine or hierarchy among the Celebrants, however all agree that the Death and Exhumation of [[Vahamut]], and to a lesser degree, the disappearance of [[Tiamat]] is worth celebrating. 

Furthermore, all Celebrants of Vahamut put great store in the older secret stories that foretell the death of [[Vahamut]]. In particular, the saint-fool-hero [[5555 Shattered Spears]] has grown in popularity among the newly forming post-[[Nachýrian Exodus|exodus]] folklore and mythology.

The beliefs espoused by the celebrants have even worked their way into new forms of [[Iomedæ]] worship. Most notably, a small but vocal denomination known as the [[Dragonriders]] has formed, that celebrate both [[Iomedæ]] and [[Vahamut]]. 

# Celebrations 
Celebrations generally take the form of long festive processions, centred around the [[Palatium]] reliquaries, which supposedly contain fragments of [[Vahamut#Exhumation|Vahamut's corpse]]. At the end of the procession, the reliquary is opened, and the fragment is used to draw a broth, which is subsequently consumed by the celebrants. 

Despite its recent origins, the Celebration of [[Vahamut]] has already had a considerable influence on the art, culture and religion of the [[Crucible]]. The music played during the processions has been used as a basis for many new [[Vanguardian|vanguardian]] music styles.
