Before the [[Tiamat#Disappearance|Disappearance of Tiamat]], and the subsequent fall of all temple hierarchy, the Temple of Tiamat was the most dominant [[Nachýrian Religion|religious institution]] in [[Nachýr]]. At its height, before the [[Crusade|Crusades]] and the [[Inquisition]], adherents could even be found outside [[Nachýr]], as far as modern day [[Lethania]] and [[Ilmbund]].

Before the [[Withering Blossoms]] and the [[Tiamat#Disappearance|Disappearance of Tiamat]], the Temple was the central bureaucratic authority of [[Nachýr]], in charge of taxation, education, and the army.

Its only holy text is the Law of [[Nachýr]], a large document that mainly deals with property and inheritance rights and regulations, that was supposedly dictated by [[Tiamat]] herself.

## Doctrine
The principle doctrine, or Þjasezvrakr (literally: Tiamat-[[God|god]]-truth.), of the Temple of [[Tiamat]] posits that the world and all it contains is the singular and undivided property of the [[God|divine]] [[Tiamat]], who resides in her capital in [[Chýrussandr]].  

The religion also teaches that any hierarchy of possessor and possessed is natural and just, and as such, that the strong have the right to rule over the weak. 

It further posits that it is the ultimate fate of the [[Nachýr|Empire]] to  spread to all nations and worlds, and save all people from the wretched states of [[Five Draconian Vices|Ignorance and Disobedience]] in which they live.

Since [[Tiamat]] was considered the final "owner" of all people and things in the world, all transactions had to be overseen by a Temple officiant. 

The temple also promoted a national [[Nachýr|Nachýrian]] identity, and moral education of its property (i.e., its citizens), most notably in the instruction in the [[Five Draconian Vices]] and by its heavily ritualized sacrificial services.

In start contrast with all other known religions of the [[Crucible]], the Temple does not posit a creation myth.

## Structure 

Before its fall, the Temple was both the Empire's dominant religious institution, and the core of its bureaucracy. As such, a large part of the temple structure revolved around the collection of various taxes, levies and tithes, in the form of goods, slaves and service, all in the service of [[Tiamat]].

The social caste that formed the highest levels of temple authority consisted entirely of [[Serpentfolk]], and conversely, all [[Serpentfolk]] were nearly without exception high functioning members of the Temple hierarchy.

At the lower levels, both [[Kovold|Kovolds]] and [[Lizardfolk|Kozarn]] could be found, especially in more rural areas.

## Downfall

The collapse of the temple started with the onset of the [[Withering Blossoms]], at some point in the mid 1760s, which rapidly eliminated all the [[Serpentfolk]] who ran the temple.

The final nail in the coffin was delivered in [[1768 AF]], with the [[Tiamat#Disappearance|Disappearance of Tiamat]], and the [[Vahamut#Exhumation|Exhumation of Vahamut]].

Ever since, the [[Celebrants of Vahamut|Celebrantion of Vahamut]] has largely displaced the Temple, except in some isolated corners of the [[Nachýr|empire]].

# Conspiracy
Within the [[Crucible]], there is a small minority of people who believe that the Temple of [[Tiamat]] is still active, and has worked together with the [[Merovianism|Merovians]] to stage the [[Nachýrian Exodus|exodus]] in an attempt to destabilize the culture of the [[Philosophical Powers]].

This idea is, however, discounted by all serious scholars.
