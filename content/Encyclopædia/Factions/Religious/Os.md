The Ora (singular: os) are (generally) priests within the [[Synodal Church of Iomedæ|Synodal Church]] who physically attend the [[Synod]] on behalf of their [[Bishop]].

While the [[Synod]] is in session, the Ora are conferred all the honours and powers of the [[Bishop]] they represent. The words they speak are even considered to have been spoken by their [[Bishop]], who is fully and personally responsible for the actions of the Os during sessions. As such, an Os is usually a very close and trusted pupil of the [[Bishop]].

When out of sessions, the Ora are beholden to all the duties of their ordinary clerical rank. 

in times of crisis and by special appointment of a [[Bishop]], laymembers have been known to hold the title of Os ad interim. 

Usually, the heir apparent to the [[Arcadia|Arcadian]] throne is granted the title of Os on behalf of the [[Mary VIII|regent]].
