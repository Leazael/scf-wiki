The serpentfolk, or _sæþvrakr_ in [[Draconic]], were, until recently, the ruling priest-nobility class that formed the core of the [[Nachýr|Nachýrian]] administration and bureaucracy. 

# Physiology
The serpentfolk were generally described as tall and lithe bipedal reptilian [[Sapient|Sapients]] with flat snake-like heads and long limbs. They are said to be significantly taller than [[Human|Humans]], with some rare individuals reaching heights in excess of 3 [[Philosophical Units|meters]] when fully stretched out.

There scales are generally described as green-grey, with sometimes flecks of gold.

Notably, the serpentfolk were said have six digits on each hand, two opposable thumbs as the first and last digit, and four regular fingers in between.

Despite their reptilian appearance, they were not related the [[Lizardfolk|Kozarn]] or [[Kovold|Kobolds]].

# Extinction
Throughout the 1760s, the serpentfolk rapidly went extinct due to a plague known as the [[Withering Blossoms]]. The power-vacuum left in their abscence has contributed to the rapid collapse of [[Nachýr|Nachýrian Empire]] , and the resulting [[Nachýrian Exodus|exodus]].
