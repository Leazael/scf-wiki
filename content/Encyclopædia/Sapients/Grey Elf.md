The Grey Elves are a particular subspecies of the more well-known [[Elf|Elves]], native to the [[Planet]] [[Elsar]]. They are commonly seen as haughty and cruel.

Grey elves have pale skin, silver or golden hair, and amber or violet eyes. Unlike High Elves, the grey elves do not change their colouration throughout their lifetimes. 

Ever since the destruction of [[Cinis]] during the [[Elven Genocide]], they have been hosts to the displaced [[Elf|Elven]] population.

Since the [[Rupture of the Æther]], the Grey Elves have considered joining the [[Shining Academy]] as one of the highest possible honours in their culture, as such many [[Bondsmage|Bondsmagi]] are Grey Elves.
