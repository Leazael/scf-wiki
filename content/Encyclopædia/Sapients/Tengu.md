Tengu are a species of corvidean [[Sapient]] that inhabit the jungles of southwest [[Nachýr]]. Small Enclaves of settled Tengu can also be found all around the [[Vareshian Commonwealth]].

Tengu are usually black feathered, with dark bead-like eyes. They are usually around 120 [[Philosophical Units|cm]] tall. Their strong feathered arms and shoulders allow them to glide small distances.
