Merfolk are a reclusive [[Sapient]] species that live underwater, and resemble [[Human|Humans]] or [[Elf|Elves]] with a long fish-like tail. Relatively little is known about them.

Merfolk have been spotted in the Five-Coloured Sea, on the coast of [[Vahestan]] and the [[Gulf of Varesh]] and it has been speculated that some species of Merfolk inhabit the [[Blightsea]].

[[Muræne|Muræne]], while superficially similar (also possessing [[Human]]-like torsos and aquatic tails), are not considered to be related to Merfolk.
