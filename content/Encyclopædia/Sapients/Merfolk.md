Merfolk are a reclusive [[Sapient]] species that live underwater, and resemble [[Human|Humans]] or [[Elf|Elves]] with long fish-like tails. Relatively little is known about them.

Merfolk have been spotted in the Five-Coloured Sea off the coast of [[Vahestan]], and in the [[Gulf of Varesh]]. Based on local folklore, it has been speculated that some species of Merfolk inhabit the [[Blightsea]]. [[Dvårgheim|Dvårgian]] sailors claim that the [[Lokastunga]] is inhabited by "menfish", though it is unknown if these creatures are proper merfolk.

[[Muræne|Muræne]], while superficially similar (also possessing [[Human]]-like torsos and aquatic tails), are not considered to be related to Merfolk.
