Orks are one of the [[Sapient|sapient]] species that can be commonly found in the [[Crucible]]. Orks are usually of an equal height with [[Human|Humans]], with powerful builds and greenish, greyish or olive skin. Their lower canine teeth often grow long enough to protrude from their mouths, and these “tusks,” combined with heavy brows and slightly pointed ears, give them, according to many humans, a bestial appearance.

The Orks of the [[Crucible]] often prefer to be called [[Half-Ork|Half-Orks]], emphasizing their [[Human]] lineage, similar to [[Half-Elf|Half-Elves]]. However, apart from cultural and societal norms, there is no biological difference between the [[Half-Ork|Half-Orks]] and the Orks from [[Las]], [[Izmoroz]] and [[Tel Varesh]]. Still, many [[Half-Ork|Half-Orks]] from the [[Crucible]] prefer the term, as, to them, it connotes civilization and refinement. In contrast, the Orks from the [[Vareshian Commonwealth|Commonwealth]] of [[Tel Varesh]] prefer to be referred to as "Orks", a fact that the people from the [[Crucible]] tend to ignore

# Demographics
Within the [[Crucible]], orks make up about 5% of the population, and up to 10% in urban areas, with higher concentrations in [[Izmoroz]], [[Las]] and [[Polovina]]. They make up about 70% of the population of [[Tel Varesh]].
