Trolls are reclusive species of [[Sapient]] that can be found in the forests of [[Izmoroz]] and [[Las]]. They are tall, strong and covered in tough and dense moss-brown, sometimes slightly greenish fur.

They generally live in small family units and are fiercely territorial, though they sometimes gather in larger raiding parties in the summer months. Throughout history, only a handful of individual trolls have been known to migrate to [[Las|Lassian]] and [[Izmoroz|Izmorozian]] cities and take up urban life.

It is said that trolls heal any injury at an astounding rate unless injured by fire.
