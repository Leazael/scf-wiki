The Murænes, sometimes called Grindylow, are an aquatic (or possibly amphibian) [[Sapient]] species, or collection of species. They inhabit the [[Wilted Ocean]] around, and possibly the lands of [[Murænia]] and the [[Tagelsea]] in particular.

They are reported to have anywhere between two and six arms, fish-like heads and tails, and resemble monstrous moray-eels. They are said to be anywhere between 1.50 and 4 [[Philosophical Units|meters]] in length.

Very little is known about them, apart from the infrequent sightings of sailors travelling along the [[Nachýr|Nachýrian]] coast, and the handful of eyewitness accounts from survivors of the [[Massacre of Eldmere]].

They have in the past been mistaken for [[Merfolk]], though their physiology differs enough that any relation is unlikely.
