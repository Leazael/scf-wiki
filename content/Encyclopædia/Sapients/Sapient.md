Sapient is a term used to describe all [[Cosmic Spheres|temporal]] species capable of sapience, that is, those that are capable of learning and wisdom. The term is generally used to make a distinction between higher species and animals.

Generally, the term sapient is also used to signify which species are capable of adopting language, morality, [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophy]] and culture. The term is also reserved for *mundane* species, that is, it excludes spirits like the [[Fair Folk]] and [[Gremlin|Gremlins]] and [[Undead]]. (Though whether it includes higher [[Automaton|Automata]] is a point of contention.)

Several different definitions of Sapient can be found depending on the context. The [[Church of Iomedæ]] generally equates sapience with having a soul and being able to acquire grace. [[Natural Philosophy|Natural Philosophers]] usually take the approach that Sapient species must intersect all [[Cosmic Spheres]].

Sapient species are generally bipedal vertebrates, with four limbs and front-facing eyes. Whether this body-plan is intrinsically linked to the state of sapience is a point of active investigation among some [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophers]].

- [[Lizardfolk]]
- [[Merfolk]]

All subspecies (like [[Grey Elf|Grey Elves]]) and hybrids (like [[Half-Elf|Half-Elves]]) are also considered sapient.
