Centaurs are a [[Sapient]] nomadic species native to the north-western planes of [[Izmoroz]]. They generally resemble fair-skinned hairy [[Human|Humans]] or [[Dwarf|Dwarves]] with the lower body of that of a horse. 

Dark-skinned Centaurs can also be found living urban lives in [[Vahestan]].

They are considered a cousin species to the [[Boazulmoš]] of [[Thrjoskarland]] and [[Nichija-Zembla]].
