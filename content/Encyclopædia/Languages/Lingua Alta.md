Lingua Alta was the language of the [[Shining Academy]]. It was the main language used for communication between the [[Bondsmage|Bondsmagi]] and is in all likelihood as old as the [[Shining Academy|Academy]] itself. 

The language was often employed by early [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophers]] and still permeates the language used by [[Alchemy|Alchemists]] and [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetic Philosophers]]. It was supposedly also used during ceremonies of the [[Guild of Stonemasons]].

The language is still taught at the [[Exalted School of Tel Varesh]], and its nature as a common language has caused a resurgence in its study, following the diplomatic contacts between the [[Crucible]] and [[Tel Varesh]].
