Sosulkan is the primary language spoken in [[Las]], [[Izmoroz]] and the north-western parts of [[Polovina]]. 

The language itself is named after the [[Izmoroz|Izmorozian]] city of [[Sosulka]] and thus has its roots in the [[Foreworld]].

Sosulkan generally uses its own script, though in parts of [[Las]] and [[Polovina]], the [[Ildarin]] script is used instead.
