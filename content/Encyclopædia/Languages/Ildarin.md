Ildarin is the primary language spoken in the countries of [[Lethania]] and [[Ilmbund]] as well as most parts of [[Polovina]].

The language is, in all likelihood, descendent from the language spoken by the people who lived in [[Ilm's Watch]] and thus has its roots in the [[Foreworld]]. Ildarin has much in common with [[Dvårgish]] and it is hypothesised that the two languages may share a common ancestor.

The language has two noteworthy dialects:
- Eldmeric (or Eldmeers), orinally spoken in the swampland closer to [[Eldmere]], though it can now be commonly be found throughout west [[Lethania]]. It is known for its harsh rolling R's and guttural G's. While this dialect used to be fairly obscure, it has grown in popularity since [[Eldmere]] became the capital of [[Lethania|South-Lethania]] in the aftermath of the [[Lethanian War of Separation]].
- Gnomic, a fast-paced dialect with a large canon of [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophical]] shorthand, spoken by the urban [[Gnome|Gnomes]].
