Dvårgish is the languages [[Dvårgheim]], though it is also spoken in [[Thrjoskarland]] and parts of [[Nichija-Zembla]].

The language itself is a mixture of the various [[Dwarf|Dwarven]] and [[Giant]] [[Ancient Languages]], once spoken on the [[Foreworld]], and was constructed to be a unifying tongue for the people of [[Dvårgheim]] when that nation was founded over 1500 years ago.

Dvårgish has much in common with [[Ildarin]], and the two probably share some common ancestor. It has probably also influenced the earlies forms of [[Anglic]] spoken in the [[Crucible]].

The days of the week used in the standard [[Calendar]] of the crucible are derived from the Dvårgish language and [[Æsir|religion]].
