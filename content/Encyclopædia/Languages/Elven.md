Elven is the language spoken by the [[Elf|Elves]]. It was supposedly the main language of [[Cinis|Eldedeël]] and is in all probability still spoken on[[Elsar]] by the [[Grey Elf|elves]] who reside there. It is one of the few known [[Ancient Languages]].

The language was still taught at the [[Shining Academy]] before it closed its gates in [[1726 AF]]. 

Some scholars have traced many lemma's in the language of [[Niyezian|Niyez]] back to elvish roots.
