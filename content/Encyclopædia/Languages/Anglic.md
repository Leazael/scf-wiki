Anglic is the primary language spoken in [[Arcadia]].

Despite the supposed [[Vahestan]] origins of the [[Arcadia|Arcadian]] people, the language has more in common with [[Dvårgish]] and [[Ildarin]], with strong [[Lingua Alta]] and minor [[Celestial]] influences.

As such, it is commonly hypothesised that the [[Arcadia|Arcadians]] may have only travelled through [[Vahestan]], without actually originating from that country. The first ancesters may have, in fact, spoken a dialect of [[Lingua Alta]]. In this case, Anglic may have arisen as a pidgin language between the [[Arcadia|Arcadian]] settlers and the [[Ilmbund|Ilmian]] locals.

 [[Anglic]] shares its alphabet with [[Ildarin]], though the diacritics used in [[Ildarin]] are commonly omitted.
