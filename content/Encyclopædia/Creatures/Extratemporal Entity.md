Extratemporal Entities are entities or intelligences that are aligned to spheres other than the [[Cosmic Spheres|Temporal Sphere]]. 

Practices of calling, binding and communion with these spirits can be found in many [[Magic|Magical]] traditions, from [[Thaumaturgy]] and the [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetic]] study of [[Transliminal Invocation]], to the exhortations made by priests of the [[Church of Iomedæ|Church]].

While these spirits are said to be _aligned_ to a particular sphere, that by no means indicates that they are contained inside it. Indeed, it is generally accepted that all [[Sapient|Sapients]] are aligned with the [[Cosmic Spheres|Temporal Sphere]], but extend across all [[Cosmic Spheres]] (see [[Extratemporal Entity#Misnomer|Misnomer]]) below.

# Examples 
Intelligences aligned to the Supernal Sphere are usually identified as the [[Angel|Angels]] from various religions, although some have postulated that only the True [[Angel|Archangels]] and [[God|gods]] hail from that Sphere.

Similarly [[Demon|Demons]] are spirits associated with the lower Spheres, and in particular the Infernal Sphere. 

[[Elemental|Elemental]] is a catch-all term for [[Extratemporal Entity|intelligences]] aligned to several different spheres. The types of elemental spirits summoned by [[Alchemy|Alchemists]] for their use in transmutations and engines usually hail from the Elementary or sometimes the Corporeal Sphere. However, the more intelligent Elemental Spirits used in [[Automaton|Automata]] are [[Transliminal Invocation|conjured]] from the Celestial and Intellectual Spheres. The sole thing these types of intelligences have in common is a strong connection to the [[Four Elements|Protean Elements]].

Besides the Elementary [[Elemental|Elementals]], there are Vital Spirits and Genii Loci that are said to inhabit the Corporeal and Elementary Spheres. Various Chthonic spirits likewise dwell in the Corporeal Sphere.

Spirits from the Intellectual Sphere are sometimes summoned as Familiars by [[Thaumaturgy|Thaumaturges]] and, less commonly, [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetic Philosophers]].

The [[Fair Folk]] are often said to be [[Cosmic Spheres|Celestially]] aligned spirits that live coterminously within the [[Cosmic Spheres|Temporal Sphere]]. Likewise, [[Gremlin|Gremlins]] may be [[Cosmic Spheres|Corporeally]] aligned spirits that extend largely into the [[Cosmic Spheres|Temporal]] and perhaps even [[Celestial]] spheres.


# Misnomer
The term "extratemporal" is, in most cases, a misnomer. Since each sphere is nested inside its superiors, all spheres lower than the [[Cosmic Spheres|Temporal Sphere]] are actually a (nested) part of the [[Cosmic Spheres|Temporal Sphere]]. Hence entities aligned with the Elementary and Infernal Sphere are not "outside" of the temporal sphere.

Likewise, entities from [[Cosmic Spheres]] superior to the [[Cosmic Spheres|Temporal Sphere]] are generally able to freely traverse the [[Cosmic Spheres|Temporal Sphere]] and its inferiors, and as such, can also not be said to be properly outside of the [[Cosmic Spheres|Temporal Sphere]].
