Cockatrices, or savlkék in [[Draconic]], are large reptilian birds with long tails frequently employed by [[Kovold|Kovolds]] as beasts of burden and mounts in battle. Their wings are leathery, and the break contains several rows of sharp teeth-like protrusions. 

They are capable of flight, even with a [[Kovold]]-rider, though they are known to be less than graceful in doing so. 

Their saliva contains a strong paralytic toxin, that causes the flesh around a bite-wound to flake and calcify.
