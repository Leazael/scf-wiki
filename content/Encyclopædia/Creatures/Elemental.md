Elementals are [[Extratemporal Entity|Extratemporal Spirits]] that embody one of the [[Four Elements|Protean Elements]], i.e., Fire, Earth, Air and Water. 

the class of elementals include a wide range of different spirits from various [[Cosmic Spheres]]. Generally, though, a distinction can be made between *base elementals* and *ascended elementals*.

# Base Elementals 
Base elementals, sometimes called *rhizomata*, are elementals hailing from the [[Cosmic Spheres|Elementary or Corporeal Spheres]]. They are always corporeal, in the sense that they possess a body of their own, are generally less subtle and sophisticated than their ascended counterparts, and generally have an animal-like level of intelligence.

These elementals are usually summoned as simple servants or power sources.

# Ascended Elementals 
Ascended elementals are elementals hailing from the [[Cosmic Spheres|Celestial and Intellectual Spheres]]. They generally possess a will and intelligence similar to a sapient's, though some are more subtle by far.

Ascended Elementals are sometimes identified by specific names according to their element. These are: Salamander (fire), Sylph (air), Nymph (water) and Oread (earth).

Ascended elementals are seldom corporeal, but instead inhabit or possess materials that are emblematic of their corresponding [[Four Elements|Element]]. Some have the ability to incorporate, though usually for a short time only.

Before the advent of [[Ordinator|Ordination]] Ascended Elementals were most often employed to assist [[Alchemy|Alchemists]] in various calculations. 

Some [[Automaton|Automata]] are powered by these intelligent elementals

# Elementals in [[Natural Philosophy]]

Elementals are most often used by [[Alchemy|Alchemists]] in order to aid in transmutations. Their close relation to the [[Four Elements|Protean Elements]] makes them indispensable in the execution of some of the more subtle transmutations. As such, the [[Ways and Means|Means]] by which elementals are conjured were first discovered by [[Alchemy|Alchemists]].

Other [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophers]] may sometimes conjure elementals to power or to power [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophical]] [[Ordinator|Engines]] or [[Automaton|Automata]]. Some [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetic Philosophers]] have also made a study of the [[Transliminal Invocation|summoning]] of Elementals as part of the studies.

When bound to an automaton or engine, the intelligence and power of the machine are closely tied with the power of the Elemental. If the spirit is too weak, it may not be very intelligent and may lack processing powers. If the elemental is too powerful, it may resent being bound and attempt to break free.

If the Elemental is capable of incorporating, it may even be able to destroy the machine from the inside.

# Deuterean Elementals
Some [[Alchemy|Alchemists]] claim that not just the [[Four Elements|Protean Elements]], but also the [[Four Elements|Deuterean Elements]] may manifest in an elemental. Such elementals would manifest as embodiments of living Iron or Sulpher and the like.

However, the [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophical]] evidence for their existence is apocryphal at best. The [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophical]] consensus being that such creatures must have been [[Gremlin|Gremlins]] of some kind instead.
