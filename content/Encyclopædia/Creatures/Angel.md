Angels are powerful [[Extratemporal Entity|Extratemporal Spirits]] native to the [[Cosmic Spheres|Heavens]]. They supposedly watch over mortals and are sometimes employed as messengers for various deities.

# In the [[Iomedæan Canon]]
In the [[Crucible]], angels are strongly associated with the [[Church of Iomedæ]]. These angels act as intermediates and messengers of [[Iomedæ]] herself and bestow her miracles on behalf of mortals that they have deemed worthy. 

Many priests in the leadership of the [[Church of Iomedæ|Churches]] have an alliance or partnership with an individual angel. Some orders are even consistently served by the same angel.

For instance, the Priest-Regent of [[Arcadia]], currently [[Mary VIII]], is usually assisted by the Archangel Remiël.

## Archangels
The dogma of the [[Synodal Church of Iomedæ|Synodal Church]], labels several angels as Archangels. These, usually four, angels are those to the [[Iomedæ|godess]], and according to the [[Three Lays|Lay of Steel]], joined her during her assault on the heavens. 

They are traditionally named Michaël, Zaphkiël, Sandalphon and Remiël, and are traditionally revered as [[Saint|Greater Saints]] of the [[Church of Iomedæ|Church]]

# [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetic]] Angels
Within [[Hermetic Philosophy]], angels are generally identified as spirits hailing from the higher [[Cosmic Spheres|Spheres]] and in particular the [[Cosmic Spheres|Supernal Sphere]]. 

Some angels have shown to be willing to be [[Transliminal Invocation|summoned]] as part of [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetic]] rituals. Most of these angels so [[Transliminal Invocation|summoned]] are said to be associated with [[Iomedæ]] and her [[Church of Iomedæ|Church]], though some may be more independent actors. 

The [[Synod]] officially opposes these practices, though their influence in the predominantly [[Asynodal Churches of Iomedæ|asynodal]] [[University|Universities]] is limited.

# Empyreal Spirits
Angels, often referred to as Empyreal Spirits, also play a role in the [[Veneration of the Dead|Ancestor Worship]] of [[Tel Varesh]], and occasionally in the practice of [[Thaumaturgy]]. 

The [[Masked Priests]] of the [[Primal Ancestors]] are known to "tame" Empyreal Spirits, and "bind" them to the service of the particular highly-placed families. Whether this is an act of coercion or whether there is some kind of deal involved is not known to the uninitiated.
