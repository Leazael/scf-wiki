Vampire, also known as _alps_ in [[Ildarin]] speaking countries, are a type of [[Undead]]. Vampires are a common bogeyman in the folklore of [[Las]] and [[Izmoroz]], and have recently emerged as a common villain in [[Sophist]] [[Kinescope|Kine-Reels]].

They are known for being nocturnal creatures that fear the [[Sun]], and are said to drink the blood of their victims. Those who are killed that way are said to turn into either vampires or [[Ghoul|ghouls]].

They are often depicted wielding a myriad of different eldritch [[Magic|magical]] powers, like [[Ensorcellment|ensorcelling]] their victims, turning into bats and summoning wolves. 

Sailors that have travelled to [[Kef Zayem]] and [[Voievodmare]] claim that vampires haunt the streets of these cities at night.
