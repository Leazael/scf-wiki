Demons, sometimes called daemons or devils, are [[Extratemporal Entity|Extratemporal Spirits]] that hail from the lowest of the [[Cosmic Spheres]].

# In the [[Iomedæan Canon]]
In the [[Crucible]], demons are nearly universally identified as evil spirits. The protection of [[Iomedæ]] is often invoked in protecting laymembers from their influence. 

While the [[Iomedæan Canon|The Canon]] has no official dogma on their origin, demons are often seen as fallen [[Angel|Angels]], or, less commonly, the corrupted souls of the departed. 

It is generally taken for fact that demons seek to corrupt the faithful, which would leave their souls as easy pickings in the afterlife. They are opposed and countered by the [[Angel|Angels]] who seek the salvation of the righteous.

## Witchcraft 
While not a part of the official dogma of the [[Synodal Church of Iomedæ|Synodal Church]], it is commonly believed that some people actively seek out the corrupting influence of demons in order to power foul magics; such people are called witches or warlocks. 

## Exorcism 
The Rite of Exorcism is one of the rites practised by the [[Church of Iomedæ|Clergy]]. During this rite, a priest invokes the power of [[Iomedæ]] to repel demons and drive them out of possessed locations or people.

# In [[Hermetic Philosophy]]
[[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetic Philosophers]] classify demons as [[Extratemporal Entity|Extratemporal Intelligences]] hailing from to the [[Cosmic Spheres|Infernal Sphere]]. Whether these intelligences are native to the [[Cosmic Spheres|Infernal Sphere]], or may, in fact, be fallen spirits (in particular [[Angel|Angels]]) from other [[Cosmic Spheres|Spheres]] is a point of [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophical]] debate.

It is an open secret that some [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetic Philosophers]] seek out contact and information from demons by various [[Transliminal Invocation|summonings]]. Such transactions generally require the [[Transliminal Invocation|Conjurer]] to offer a boon from one of the [[Cosmic Spheres|Higher Spheres]], typically in the form of [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetically]] Enriched [[Miasma]], though other, stranger, deals are not unheard of. 

The constructs and invocations used in [[Transliminal Invocation]] are usually steeped in the trappings of [[Esoteric Iomedæism]], and frequently invoke the [[Iomedæ|godess]] and her [[Angel|Angels]] for protective purposes.

The [[Synod]] officially opposes the practice of [[Transliminal Invocation|Summoning]] Demons, though their influence in the predominantly [[Asynodal Churches of Iomedæ|asynodal]] [[University|Universities]] is limited.
