The [[Lethania|Lethanian]]-[[Vareshian Commonwealth|Vareshian]] expeditions refer to a series of four military naval expeditions undertaken against the [[Muræne|Murænes]] near the islands of [[Murænia]].

These expeditions took place between [[1756 AF]] and [[[1769 AF]], and were meant as a retaliation for the [[Massacre of Eldmere]] and similar attacks on [[Tel Varesh|Vareshian]] soil.

# Prelude
The history of trade between the [[Vareshian Commonwealth]] and [[Lethania]] goes back more than a hundred years, to even before the founding of the [[Lethanian Colonies]] in [[1614 AF]]. 

Throughout this time, up until the 1740s, sightings of [[Muræne|Murænes]] were infrequent and apocryphal. 

However, in the 1740s and 1750s, trade between the two nations saw a significant uptake. Partially due to [[Wirtship|wirtish]] factors, and partially due to innovations in [[Miasma]]-powered sea travel, traffic through the [[Tagelsea]] increased by almost an order of magnitude.

It is generally agreed that this increased traffic is what provoked the [[Muræne|Murænes]] into attacking.

# [[Massacre of Eldmere]]
On the 17th of the [[Calendar|Frostmonth]] [[1754 AF]], shortly after sundown, the [[Muræne|Murænes]] and their forces came out of the river [[Thryr]], and attacked the [[Lethania|Lethanian]] capital of [[Eldmere]].

Before the sun came up, many thousands were dead, with casualties into the tens of thousands. Before any reinforcements could arrive, they withdrew back into the river disappeared.

Later it was reported that in that same night, similar, though less successful, attacks took place in [[Zaar Godot]] and [[Kef Zayem]].

# Response 
The unprovoked [[Massacre of Eldmere|onslaught]] helped only to reinforce the ties between the [[Lethania|Lethanians]] and [[Tel Varesh|Vareshians]], and before the year was over formal embassies had been opened in [[Eldmere]] and [[Tel Varesh]].

The two nations agreed to work together to retaliate against the [[Muræne|Murænes]], in the form a joint military naval expedition to the [[Muræne|Murænes]] supposed [[Murænia|home country]].

# First Expedition
The first expedition was launched in the spring of [[1756 AF]], and consisted of:
- Eight [[Tel Varesh|Vareshian]] frigates.
- A [[Tel Varesh|Vareshian]] ship of the line.
- Three [[Lethania|Lethanian]] ironclads.
- Two [[Lethania|Lethanian]] torpedo boats.
- A [[Lethania|Lethanian]] dreadnought.
- Several small [[Lethania|Lethanian]] submersibles. 

This first expedition was able to roughly map the islands comprising [[Murænia]], and managed to catch sight of the undersea structure dubbed [[Murænia|Murænenstadt]]. 

The expedition encountered no resistance, until on the 13th night three frigates and both torpedo boats sank into the black sea. The expedition was halted soon after.

# Second Expedition
The second expedition was launched just after [[Yule Feast|newyear]] in [[1759 AF]], and consisted of:
- Three [[Tel Varesh|Vareshian]] Ships of the Line.
- Seven [[Tel Varesh|Vareshian]] frigates.
- Five [[Lethania|Lethanian]] ironclads.
- One [[Lethania|Lethanian]] dreadnought.
- Three [[Lethania|Lethanian]] [[Aviation|Ærostats]].
- One [[Lethania|Lethanian]] submarine.

The second expedition was able to more precisely map the [[Murænia|Murænian Isles]]. One of the [[Aviation|Ærostats]] even attempted a brief land survey. However, after landing contact was swiftly lost. 

Simultaneously, the submarine attempted to chart the layout of [[Murænia|Murænenstadt]], however, it sank before it could send a proper distress call.

In response, numerous [[Alchemy|Alchemical]]] depth-charges above [[Murænia|Murænenstadt]] were released, however these charges were intercepted by something and never detonated properly.

The expedition was halted on the recommendation of the [[Tel Varesh|Vareshian]] [[Thaumaturgy|Thaumaturges]] who predicted the arrival of heavy and unnatural storms.

# Third Expedition
The second expedition was launched in the summer of in [[1761 AF]], and consisted of the same setup as the second expedition, with the addition of three more [[Lethania|Lethanian]] submarines.

During the third expedition, the [[Lethania|Lethanian]] naval [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophers]] managed to collaborate with the [[Tel Varesh|Vareshian]] [[Thaumaturgy|Thaumaturges]] to protect the depth-charges from the interference of the [[Muræne|Murænes]].

As such, the third expedition successfully managed to damage some of the structures comprising [[Murænia|Murænenstadt]]. Simultaneously, the [[Aviation|Ærostats]] dropped several tons of bombs on the settlements visible on the isles. 

In the aftermath, supposedly several bodies of [[Muræne|Murænes]] were recovered. These, however, decomposed quickly and due to  fear of disease, were disposed of before they could reach the shore.

# Fourth Expedition
As there was no retaliation since the third expedition, a fourth, smaller expedition was launched in the summer of [[1764 AF]]. This expedition consisted of only:
- One [[Tel Varesh|Vareshian]] Ship of the Line.
- Two [[Tel Varesh|Vareshian]] frigates.
- Three [[Lethania|Lethanian]] ironclads.
- One [[Lethania|Lethanian]] dreadnought.
- Three [[Lethania|Lethanian]] [[Aviation|Ærostats]].
- One [[Lethania|Lethanian]] submarine.

This expedition repeated its previous method of delivering [[Thaumaturgy|Thaumaturgically]] protected depth charges. Several of these hit their target, but others detonated far above the sea floor. 

The expedition was aborted after several days of repeated bombing attempts for unknown reasons. 

# Aftermath
The consensus is that the expeditions have been successful in warning the [[Muræne|Murænes]] not to interfere with [[Lethania|Lethanian]] and [[Tel Varesh|Vareshian]] trade ships. However, many are left wondering if the expeditions had any effect, and whether they truly work as a deterrent against another [[Massacre of Eldmere|massacre]].

What is undisputed is that the expeditions have greatly tightened the relations between the [[Crucible]] and the [[Vareshian Commonwealth|Commonwealth]], to the point that [[Tel Varesh|Vareshian]] culture has become somewhat in vogue in parts of [[Lethania]]. 

Furthermore, the widely publicised collaboration between the [[Natural Philosophy|Philosophers]] and [[Thaumaturgy|Thaumaturges]] have led to an increased awareness of the importance of developing new [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetic]] methods of warfare.
