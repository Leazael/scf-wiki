The Massacre of Eldmere is the name for the [[Murænia|Murænian]] attack on [[Eldmere]] on Freysday the 17th of the [[Calendar|Frostmonth]] [[1754 AF]].

The direct cause of the massacre is unknown, although many assume that the increased traffic between [[Tel Varesh]], [[Lethania]] and [[Vareshian Colonies|their]] [[Lethanian Colonies|colonies]] through the [[Tagelsea]] in the 1740s and 1750s may have somehow provoked the [[Muræne|Murænes]] into attacking. (More information can be found [[Lethanian-Vareshian Expeditions|here]].)

The attack itself started just after sundown, when, according to eyewitness accounts, the _things_ came out of the river [[Thryr]]. Whether the assailants were the [[Muræne|Murænes]] themselves, [[Undead]] or other monsters is unknown, as the accounts of what exactly transpired vary wildly. 

It is generally assumed that the attack included some form of induced [[Ensorcellment|mass hallucination]], a hypothesis endorsed by [[Vareshian Commonwealth|Vareshian]] [[Thaumaturgy|Thaumaturges]], who have studied the [[Muræne|Murænes]] and their [[Magic|magics]].

Throughout the night, fires spread through the city, and many thousands of people were either killed or taken by the [[Muræne|Murænes]], leaving thousands of people either killed, or presumed dead as no bodies could be found. It is speculated that some of the slain may have [[Necromancy|risen]] as [[Undead]], and later retreated back into the river, with the attackers.

The attack ended at sunrise, when whatever attack the city withdrew back into the river. In the chaos and mass-hysteria, it took weeks to tally the dead and missing: almost 6000 in total, with many tens of thousands wounded or injured.

# Aftermath 
In the aftermath of the massacre, it was for a long time unclear who or what was behind the attack. Early theories pointed towards [[Nachýr]], or even the [[Bondsmage|Bondsmagi]] (who by then had been gone for nearly three decades).

Confirmation that it was indeed the [[Muræne|Murænes]] came almost two weeks after the attack, when the news spread that on that night similar (though less successful) attacks had taken place in the [[Vareshian Commonwealth|Vareshian]] cities of [[Zaar Godot]] and [[Kef Zayem]].

These attacks quickly led the [[Lethania|Lethanian Republic]] and the [[Vareshian Commonwealth]], at that time only trading partners, to form a new military alliance. Before the year was over, embassies had been opened in [[Eldmere]] and [[Tel Varesh]], and the plans for [[Lethanian-Vareshian Expeditions|a retaliation mission]] were agreed upon. 

Within 18 months, the two nations would embark on a series of [[Lethanian-Vareshian Expeditions|expeditions]] to locate the homeland of the [[Muræne|Murænes]], and to carry out retaliations for the attack.

The massacre also triggered a increased public and political interest in the study of [[Hermetic Philosophy]]. Before the attack, the study of [[Hermetic Philosophy]] had been a largely academic affair. However, to many the massacre caused an immediate stimulus for the creation of [[Hermetic Philosophy|Hermetic]] means of defence and, ultimately, warfare. As such, the [[Academy of Weißenfels]] opened its department dedicated to the study of [[Hermetic Philosophy]] not much later, in [[1757 AF]].
