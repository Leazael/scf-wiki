After the death of Priest-King [[James IV]] and his succession by Priest-Queen [[Mary VIII]] in [[1737 AF]], many pro-apocalyptic reforms were passed. Unfortunately, these reforms led to the secession of several more conservative church members from [[Las]] and [[Izmoroz]], who united as the [[Sosulkan Churches of Iomedæ]], an event often called the [[Secession of '37]]. The remaining [[Arcadia|Arcadian]], [[Polovina|Polovian]] and [[Lethania|Lethanian]] [[Bishop|Bishops]] have been dubbed the [[Orthodox Synodal Church of Iomedæ]].

The Reparation of '51 refers to the reunification of the [[Sosulkan Churches of Iomedæ]] with the [[Orthodox Synodal Church of Iomedæ]] within the [[Synodal Church of Iomedæ]]. This occurred in the wake of the death of [[Koschei the Deathless]] and his succession by [[Ivan I Czarovitch]].

As part of the reparation, the number of [[Bishop|Bishops]] representing the [[Sosulkan Churches of Iomedæ|Sosulkan Churches]] in the [[Synod]] has been formally agreed to be equal to the number of supplied by the [[Orthodox Synodal Church of Iomedæ]], 24 of each.
