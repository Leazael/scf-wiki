The Ilmian Revolution was an internal revolution within the old [[Ilmbund|kingdom of Ilm]] in [[1423 AF]], that led to the founding of the Republic of [[Ilmbund]].

# Cause
The generally accepted cause for the Revolution was the various controversies surrounding the Royal House of [[Ilmbund|Ilm]] throughout the  [[Crusade|Third Crusade]].

In particular, the Royal House, who were already despised for their decadence, allegedly tried to sell its steel and iron to both sides of the conflict. This ultimately brought down the contempt of the common people on the Royal House.

# Revolution
The people of [[Ilmbund|Ilm]], supported by the [[Arcadia|Arcadian]] [[Synod]], rebelled against the Royal House in the Revolt of [[1423 AF]]. 

After three decades of internal struggle in [[1455 AF]], the power-vacuum was filled by the Triumvirate, a joined council created by the three [[Great Guilds of Ilmbund]] (Being the Blacksmiths, the Stonemasons and the Carpenters). The nation was redubbed [[Ilmbund]] and its largest city, [[Sälen]], was proclaimed its capital.
