The Twelve-Day-War was a series of [[Revelationary Politics|Apocalyptic]] riots, protests and clashes with the [[Sälen]] constabulary in the summer of [[1730 AF]], that are generally recognized as the starting point of the [[Revelationary Revolution]].

During this twelve-day event, the halls of the [[Great Guilds of Ilmbund]] were stormed and occupied by an angry mob of citizens. It ended after two of the guilds, the [[Guild of Alchemists]] and the [[Guild of Clockworkers]] agreed to the demands for [[Revelationary Politics|Revelation]].  

In its wake, the autocracy of the [[Triumvirate of Ilmbund]] was dismantled, a parliament was instated and limited suffrage (i.e., for all adults with a guild membership) was adopted.
