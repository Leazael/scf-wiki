Then Rupture of the Æther was a cataclysmic event that took place somewhere between 2500 and 3000 years ago, at least seven centuries before the common [[Calendar|counting of years]].

During this event, the [[Foreworld]] was either destroyed or reshaped (or both) and the [[Planet|Planets]], as they currently exist, were formed.

Very few accounts of that event have survived, with the [[Three Lays]] of [[Iomedæ]], documenting her Assault on the [[Cosmic Spheres|Heavens]], being one of the few supposed exceptions. 

The [[Bondsmage|Bondsmagi]] of the [[Shining Academy]] have always claimed to have played a large role in rebuilding the [[Planet|Planets]] in the wake of the Rupture. As such, they have traditionally counted the [[Calendar|Years]] in Anno Restitutionis.

Despite the common name the "Æther" named here has very little to do with [[Luminiferous Æther]] or [[Caloric Æther]]. Instead it more likely refers to the structure of the [[Cosmic Spheres]].
