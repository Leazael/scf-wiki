Before the [[Lethanian War of Separation]], the Great Joust was an annual event, traditionally held during the [[Yule Feast]], where the six Great Houses of (the still unified) [[Lethania]] fought to determine their respective leaders, as well as the First-Among-Equals of the nation. The joust was fought mounted on the Great Mounts of each family. 

While ostensibly a fair competition, in practice, the results for intra-house primacy were generally rigged before hand. Only the First-Among-Equals position was usually up for legitimate trial. 

The "Great Joust" has also been used as a metonym to describe the collected Great Houses, then consisting of being:
- [[House Arkys]] of [[Hèrlmersc]]
- [[House Bern]] of [[Væringard]]
- [[House Eburin]] of [[Thurandir]]
- [[House Denris]] of [[Fenwald]]
- [[House Brightshield]] of [[Averion]]
- [[House Winselniß]] of [[Yrsingen]]

The last truely "great" Joust was fought in [[1740 AF]]. Its winner, [[Vladimir Denris]] of [[Fenwald]], attempted to use his title of First-Among-Equals, to revert some of the [[Revelationary Revolution|apocalyptic]] changes the southern provinces had instituted. This outcome is considered the primary casus belli of the [[Lethanian War of Separation]].


In modern [[Revelationary Politics|post-apocalyptic]] [[Lethania|South-Lethania]], the Joust is still celebrated, though the very real jousting has been replaced by local re-enactments, or even mockeries (especially of the victory of [[Vladimir Denris]], of famous past jousts. 

In [[Polovina]] the great joust is still annually held, and it is consistently won by the Wyvern-mounted [[House of Denris-Bern|United House of Denris-Bern]]. Its winner is appointed the title of High-Marshal of the [[Polovina|Polovian]] army.
