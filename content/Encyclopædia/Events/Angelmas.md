The feast of Angelmas (_Engelmis_ in [[Ildarin]]), or the Feast of the Angels, is an [[Church of Iomedæ|Iomedæn]] festival, observed in most [[Synodal Church of Iomedæ|Synodal]] and [[Asynodal Churches of Iomedæ|Asynodal]] traditions, celebrated on the Sun's Eve before the last Sunday of the [[Calendar|Winemonth]].

The festival celebrates the [[Angel|Angels]] that assisted [[Iomedæ]] in her assault on the heavens. 

As it often falls close to the autumnal equinox, it is often used to mark the beginning of autumn, and the shortening of days.

# Angelseve and [[Calendar#Leap Years|Leap Year]]
The Freysday before Angelmas is also called Angelseve (or _Eng'lenavond_ in [[Ildarin]]). Angelseve is a day traditionally used for the collection of taxes, the settling of accounts and the reckoning of debts. 

Every 28 years, on [[Calendar#Leap Years|Leap Year]], Angelseve is skipped entirely, meaning that Thorsday the 25th is followed directly by Sunseve the 27th without the Freysday in between. Note that, since [[Calendar#Leap Years|Leap Years]] occur every 28 years, these dates are the same for every [[Calendar#Leap Years|Leap Year]].

Traditionally, debts that would have been settled on the skipped Angelseve are forgiven, rent is skipped and taxes remain uncollected.
