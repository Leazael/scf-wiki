The [[Nachýr|Nachýrian]] Exodus is a mass migration, comprising mainly [[Kovold|Kovolds]], [[Lizardfolk|Kozarn]] and [[Human|Humans]] from [[Nachýr]] into the eastern [[Philosophical Powers]]. While the Exodus technically started in late [[1768 AF]], it did not gain momentum until early [[1769 AF]], when it started in earnest. 

It is estimated that between 600,000 and 1,800,000 migrants have taken part in the Exodus so far. Many of these have moved to large slums constructed on the outskirts of the major [[Philosophical Powers|Philosophical cities]].

# Cause 
The primary cause is the collapse of the [[Nachýr|Nachýrian]] power structure, and the effective destruction of the [[Nachýr|Nachýrian]] economy. 

This was itself caused by the extinction of the [[Nachýr|Nachýrian]] [[Serpentfolk|ruling class]] by the [[Withering Blossoms]], as well as the [[Tiamat#Disappearance|disappearance of [[Tiamat]] and the coinciding [[Vahamut#Exhumation|Exhumation of Vahamut]].

The Exodus is said by some to be divinely inspired by the latter event, especially the many [[Celebrants of Vahamut]] which comprise many, if not most, migrants. 

# Consequences 
The Exodus has caused much political turmoil among the [[Crucible]] as political powers struggle to deal with the consequences of the mass migration. Of those countries, [[Lethania]], [[Izmoroz]], [[Ilmbund]] and [[Arcadia]] are most strongly affected. Only [[Polovina]] has managed to keep the Exodus out, but only by applying a great amount of deadly force. 

The culture-clash in the wake of the Exodus has had a profound influence on the art, culture, politics and even [[Dragonriders|religion]] of the [[Philosophical Powers]]. Many political parties were forced to take up political positions on how to deal with the mass migration. 

[[Nachýr|Nachýrian]] music and culture has been enthusiastically adopted by several [[Vanguardian]] groups, which has led to a sudden influx of new musical styles since the Exodus.
