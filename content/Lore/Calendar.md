The most common Calendar in use in both the [[Lethania]], [[Polovina]], [[Ilmbund]], [[Las]], [[Dvårgheim]] and [[Arcadia]] is the Foundational Calender, described below.

[[Izmoroz]] used to have a slightly different system, however, since the rule of [[Ivan I Czarovitch]], [[Sosulka]] and other large [[Izmoroz|Izmorozian]] cities have started to adopt the "modern" Foundational Calendar.

# Years
The years are counted starting at [[0 AF]] or _Anno Fundationis_ in [[Lingua Alta]], the year of the [[Great Joust]] that resulted in the founding of [[Lethania]]. 

Each year, comprising roughly 360 days, is divided into four seasons, namely winter, spring, summer and autumn, which are themselves subdivided into months (See Below) of 30 days. Alternatively, the year is also subdivided into roughly 51 weeks of seven days each. 

# Months

# Seasons 
Usually, the year is divided into four seasons, of which summer and winter last four months each, and with spring and autumn lasting two each.

The solstices and equinoxes occur halfway through their respective seasons, with relatively little seasonal lag.

# Days
The week can be divided into seven days, mainly named after the [[Æsir]], even in countries where the worship of [[Iomedæ]] is more common.

The names, or variants thereof, for the weekdays are used in all [[Anglic]], [[Dvårgish]] and [[Ildarin]] speaking countries ([[Arcadia]], [[Dvårgheim]], [[Lethania]], [[Polovina]], [[Ilmbund]]). [[Sosulkan]] speaking countries use a similar seven-day system, albeit with differing etymologies. 

Using the numbering above, the day of the week $w$ can be calculated from the date as follows:
$$
w \equiv 3+3y+2m+d \mod 7,
$$
where $y$ is the year, $m$ the month and $d$ the day. Throughout the 1700s and 1770s, this simplifies to
$$
w \equiv 3z+2m+d \mod 7,
$$
where $z = y - 1700$ or $z = y-1770$ is the number made from the last two digits (for the 1700s), or the last digit (in the case of the 1770s) of the year.


# Leap Years
Most years are Common Years of 360 days, however, every 28th year, starting from [[28 AF]], is a Leap Year, which consists of only 359 days. 

In [[Arcadia]], [[Lethania]], [[Polovina]], [[Ilmbund]] and parts of [[Las]], the day skipped is the Freysday before [[Angelmas]], i.e, the Freysday before the last Sunday of the Winemonth. This day is one on which taxes and debts are traditionally collected, hence the leap year is traditionally associated with debt forgiveness and tax relief.

As Leap Years occur every 28 years, and since there are 7 days in the week, the date on which the Leap-Day occurs is the same for every such year: namely the 26th of the Winemonth. This means that on Leap Years, Thorsday the 25th is directly followed by Sunseve the 27th without the Freysday in between.
