1764 AF was a [[Calendar#Leap Years|Leap Year]] starting on [[Calendar|Sunseve]]. It was the 1764th year following the founding of [[Lethania]], and lasted from 2479 AR until 2480 AR.

# Notable Events
- [[Lethania]] and the [[Vareshian Commonwealth]] cease their war against the [[Muræne|Murænes]], following their fourth [[Lethanian-Vareshian Expeditions|expidition]].
