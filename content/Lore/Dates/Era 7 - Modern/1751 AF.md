1751 AF was a [[Calendar|Common Year]] starting on [[Calendar|Týrsday]]. It was the 1751st year following the founding of [[Lethania]], and lasted from 2466 AR until 2467 AR.

# Notable Events
- [[Arcadia]] establishes its first [[Arcadian Colonies|Colony]] in [[Vahestan]].
- The Western Bishops of the [[Sosulkan Churches of Iomedæ]] rejoin the [[Synod]], as part of the [[Reparation of '51]].
- [[Lethania]] first coms into contact with the [[Muræne|Murænes]].
