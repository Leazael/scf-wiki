1765 AF was a [[Calendar|Common Year]] starting on [[Calendar|Týrsday]]. It was the 1765th year following the founding of [[Lethania]], and lasted from 2480 AR until 2481 AR.

# Notable Events
- Word of the [[Withering Blossoms]] that plague the [[Serpentfolk]] of [[Nachýr]] reaches the [[Crucible]].
