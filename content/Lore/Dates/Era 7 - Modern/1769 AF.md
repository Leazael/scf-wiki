1769 AF was a [[Calendar|Common Year]] starting on [[Calendar|Sunday]]. It was the 1769th year following the founding of [[Lethania]], and lasted from 2484 AR until 2485 AR.

# Notable Events
- [[Polovina]] fortifies its borders against the oncoming [[Kovold]] [[Nachýrian Exodus|invasion]]. 
- The [[Nachýrian Exodus]] exodus reaches [[Ilmbund]] and [[Arcadia]].
