1746 AF was a [[Calendar|Common Year]] starting on [[Calendar|Moonsday]]. It was the 1746th year following the founding of [[Lethania]], and lasted from 2461 AR until 2462 AR.

# Notable Events
- [[Dvårgheim]] proclaims [[Ragnarǫk]], believing that the [[Cacotopic Warp]] is a herald of the end-times.
