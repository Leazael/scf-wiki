1768 AF was a [[Calendar|Common Year]] starting on [[Calendar|Thorsday]]. It was the 1768th year following the founding of [[Lethania]], and lasted from 2483 AR until 2484 AR.

# Notable Events
- [[Tiamat]] disappears, and [[Vahamut|Vahamut's Corpse]] is discovered.
- The [[Nachýrian Exodus]] begins.
