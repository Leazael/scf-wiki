1766 AF was a [[Calendar|Common Year]] starting on [[Calendar|Freysday]]. It was the 1766th year following the founding of [[Lethania]], and lasted from 2482 AR until 2483 AR.

# Notable Events
- A [[Astrology|Planetary Alignment]] occurred on the last day of the Grassmonth, the first full Alignment since the supposed reshaping of the planets in [[0 AR]].
