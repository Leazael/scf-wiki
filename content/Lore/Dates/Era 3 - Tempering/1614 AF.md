1614 AF was a [[Calendar|Common Year]] starting on [[Calendar|Thorsday]]. It was the 1614th year following the founding of [[Lethania]], and lasted from 2329 AR until 2330 AR.

# Notable Events
- The first [[Lethanian Colonies|Lethanian Colony]] is purchesed from [[Tiamat]], and [[Fort Arkys]] is established.
