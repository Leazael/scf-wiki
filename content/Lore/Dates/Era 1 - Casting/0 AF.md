0 AF the Year in which [[Lethania]] was founded. It lasted from 715 AR until 716 AR.

# Notable Events
- The first [[Great Joust]] is held and the country of [[Lethania]] is founded.
- The new [[Calendar]], marking the years in _Anno Fundationis_ are introduced: the first day after the [[Yule Feast]] will become the first day of [[1 AF]].
