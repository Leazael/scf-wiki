1744 AF was a [[Calendar|Common Year]] starting on [[Calendar|Týrsday]]. It was the 1744th year following the founding of [[Lethania]], and lasted from 2459 AR until 2460 AR.

# Notable Events
- Third year of the [[Lethanian War of Separation]].
- [[Klingeborn]] is taken by [[Dvårgheim|Dvårgian]] forces.
- A second trench-front forms between [[Thurandir]] and [[Yrsingen]].
- The [[Corridor]] nations officially ban the [[Synodal Church of Iomedæ|Synodal Church]]. Most churches officially become [[Asynodal Churches of Iomedæ|Asynodal]].
- The [[Holy Order of Paladins]] conquers [[Maidenshrine]].
