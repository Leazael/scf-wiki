1704 AF was a [[Calendar|Common Year]] starting on [[Calendar|Moonsday]]. It was the 1704th year following the founding of [[Lethania]], and lasted from 2419 AR until 2420 AR.

# Notable Events
- The [[Guild of Alchemists]] replaces the Guild of Blacksmiths in the [[Triumvirate of Ilmbund|Ilmian Triumvirate]].
