1711 AF was a [[Calendar|Common Year]] starting on [[Calendar|Moonsday]]. It was the 1711th year following the founding of [[Lethania]], and lasted from 2426 AR until 2427 AR.

# Notable Events
- The [[Guild of Clockworkers]] replaces the Guild of Carpenters in the [[Triumvirate of Ilmbund|Ilmian Triumvirate]].
