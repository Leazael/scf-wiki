1729 AF was a [[Calendar|Common Year]] starting on [[Calendar|Sunseve]]. It was the 1729th year following the founding of [[Lethania]], and lasted from 2444 AR until 2445 AR.

# Notable Events
- Peace is enforced between [[Nachýr]] and [[Arcadia]] by [[Lethania]]. 
- The [[Step-Stone Isles]] come under [[Lethania|Lethanian]] dominion.
