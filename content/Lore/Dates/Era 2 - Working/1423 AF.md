1423 AF was a [[Calendar|Common Year]] starting on [[Calendar|Freysday]]. It was the 1423rd year following the founding of [[Lethania]], and lasted from 2138 AR until 2139 AR.

# Notable Events
- The seventh year of the [[Crusade|Third Crusade]]
- The [[Ilmian Revolution]] occurs, and the King of [[Ilmbund|Ilm]] is deposed.
