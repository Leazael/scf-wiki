1738 AF was a [[Calendar|Common Year]] starting on [[Calendar|Freysday]]. It was the 1738th year following the founding of [[Lethania]], and lasted from 2453 AR until 2454 AR.

# Notable Events
- [[Arcadia]] holds its first general election for its new parliament in [[Beorminster]].
- [[Arora van Finsterling|Prof. van Finsterling]] opens the first department dedicated to the study of [[Hermetic Philosophy]] at the [[College of Elbisborg]].
