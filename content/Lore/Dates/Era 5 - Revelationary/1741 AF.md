1741 AF was a [[Calendar|Common Year]] starting on [[Calendar|Sunday]]. It was the 1741st year following the founding of [[Lethania]], and lasted from 2456 AR until 2457 AR.

# Notable Events
- The [[Great Joust]] of [[Lethania]] is won by the aggressively anti-[[Revelationary Politics|Apocalyptic]] [[Vladimir Denris]] of [[Fenwald]].
- [[Vladimir Denris]] tries to force the southern [[Lethania|Lethanian]] provinces to revert their [[Revelationary Revolution|Apocalyptic]] reforms.
