1740 AF was a [[Calendar|Common Year]] starting on [[Calendar|Thorsday]]. It was the 1740th year following the founding of [[Lethania]], and lasted from 2455 AR until 2456 AR.

# Notable Events
- The [[Great Joust]] of [[Lethania]] is won by the aggressively anti-[[Revelationary Politics|Apocalyptic]] [[Vladimir Denris]] of [[Fenwald]].
