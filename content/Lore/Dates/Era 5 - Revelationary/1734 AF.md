1734 AF was a [[Calendar|Common Year]] starting on [[Calendar|Sunday]]. It was the 1734th year following the founding of [[Lethania]], and lasted from 2449 AR until 2450 AR.

# Notable Events
- Several real-estate related corruption scandals within the [[Synod]] and the [[Synodal Church of Iomedæ|Synodal Church]] are exposed.
