1735 AF was a [[Calendar|Common Year]] starting on [[Calendar|Midweek]]. It was the 1735th year following the founding of [[Lethania]], and lasted from 2450 AR until 2451 AR.

# Notable Events
- The [[White Revolution]] occurs.
  - With many more people joining various new [[Asynodal Churches of Iomedæ|Asynodal Churches]], the remaining faithful to the [[Synodal Church of Iomedæ|Synodal Church]] gather in the streets of [[Covenant]].
  - These pro-[[Revelationary Revolution|Apocalyptic]] protesters are violently supressed by [[James IV]], with the aid of undercover agents of the [[Holy Order of St. Alia]].
